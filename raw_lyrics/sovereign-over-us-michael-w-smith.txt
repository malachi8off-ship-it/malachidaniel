Sovereign Over Us
Michael W. Smith
meaning
This worship anthem brings comfort by declaring that even in tragedy, God's plans are for our good and His glory. It is deeply anchored in the story of Joseph in Genesis 50:20, declaring that what the enemy meant for evil, God meant for good.
