This Is Your Time (Columbine)
Michael W. Smith
meaning
This song was written to honor a victim of the Columbine tragedy, encouraging believers to boldly live out their faith. It reflects the biblical call in Esther 4:14 to step into purpose, realizing we are called 'for such a time as this.'
