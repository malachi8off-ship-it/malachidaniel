Take It All Back (Official Music Video)
Tauren Wells
meaning
"Take It All Back" is a contemporary worship anthem by Tauren Wells that invites believers to surrender every aspect of their lives to God's sovereign love and purpose. The lyrics echo biblical themes of repentance, restoration, and the believer’s identity in Christ, drawing on passages such as Psalm 51 and Ephesians 1:7, while encouraging a wholehearted commitment to God’s redemptive plan. The official music video visually reinforces this call to relinquish personal agendas and embrace God's transformative grace.
