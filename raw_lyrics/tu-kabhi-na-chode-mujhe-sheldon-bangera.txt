Tu Kabhi Na Chode Mujhe
Sheldon Bangera
meaning
Sung in Hindi, this comforting track rests on the absolute promise that God will never abandon His children. It is a direct application of the biblical assurance found in Hebrews 13:5, 'Never will I leave you; never will I forsake you.'
