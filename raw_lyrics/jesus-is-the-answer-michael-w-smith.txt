Jesus Is The Answer
Michael W. Smith
meaning
This classic gospel-style song proclaims that amidst all of life's confusion and darkness, Jesus remains the only true light and solution. It reflects the biblical assurance that Christ is the light of the world that darkness cannot overcome.
