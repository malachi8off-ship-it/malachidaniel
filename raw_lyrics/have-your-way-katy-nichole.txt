Have Your Way
Katy Nichole
meaning
This song is a prayer of complete surrender, asking God to take control of the singer's life and plans. It mirrors the ultimate prayer of submission Jesus prayed in the Garden of Gethsemane: 'Not my will, but yours be done.'
