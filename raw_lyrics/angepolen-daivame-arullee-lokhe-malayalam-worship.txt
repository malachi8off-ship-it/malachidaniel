Angepolen Daivame Arullee Lokhe
Malayalam Worship
meaning
This Christian worship song offers a profound theological reflection on absolute trust and exclusive devotion to God, identifying Jesus Christ as the worshiper's sole refuge and all-in-all, resonating with biblical declarations of God's uniqueness and trustworthiness (e.g., Isaiah 45, Psalm 62). It moves from deep adoration to a prayer of complete surrender, seeking purification by His Word, guidance by His will, and empowerment by the Holy Spirit, reflecting core New Testament principles of discipleship and sanctification (e.g., Ephesians 5). The central theme is fervent worship born out of total reliance and commitment to spiritual transformation.
