Angepolen Daivame Arullee Lokhe
Malayalam Worship
meaning
This Christian worship song profoundly expresses radical adoration, complete surrender, and total dependence on God, specifically Jesus Christ, acknowledging Him as incomparable and the sole refuge and everything. It echoes themes from Psalms regarding divine uniqueness and trust, while also articulating a deep desire for spiritual cleansing by God's Word, guidance by His will, and empowering by the Holy Spirit. The overarching theme is worship and personal consecration, inviting believers to yield fully to Christ's Lordship.
