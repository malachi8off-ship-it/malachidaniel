God Problems
Maverick City Music & Naomi Raine
meaning
This song encourages believers to surrender impossible situations and anxieties to God, recognizing that only He can solve them. It aligns with the biblical promise in 1 Peter 5:7 to cast all your anxiety on Him because He cares for you.
