Same God
Elevation Worship
meaning
'Same God' by Elevation Worship is a declarative worship song affirming God's immutable nature and consistent faithfulness throughout history. It grounds its core meaning in Old Testament narratives, referencing the miraculous acts of God for patriarchs like Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob, thereby establishing a biblical context of divine power and covenant keeping. The song ultimately reassures believers that the same mighty God of the past remains actively present and wholly reliable in their lives today.
