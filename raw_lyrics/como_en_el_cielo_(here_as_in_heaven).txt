Como en el Cielo (Here as in Heaven)
Elevation Worship
meaning
"Como en el Cielo" ("Here as in Heaven") by Elevation Worship is a contemporary Christian worship song that centers on the theological concept of bringing God's heavenly kingdom, will, and presence into the present earthly reality. Musically and lyrically, the track reflects themes of spiritual awakening, unity among believers, and the active manifestation of divine power on Earth. Its core message and title are directly inspired by the Lord's Prayer found in Matthew 6:10, specifically the petition, "Your kingdom come, your will be done, on earth as it is in heaven."
