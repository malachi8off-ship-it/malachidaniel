Aarumillennakilum Enikkashrayamillenkilum
Abhijith Kollam
meaning
Sung in Malayalam, this worship song expresses complete reliance on Jesus Christ during times of loneliness and deep isolation. The theme emphasizes finding refuge in God when all earthly support systems fail, reflecting the biblical context of Psalm 46:1, where God is declared as an ever-present help and refuge in trouble.
