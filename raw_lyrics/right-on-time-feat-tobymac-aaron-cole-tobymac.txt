Right on Time (feat. Tobymac)
Aaron Cole, TobyMac
meaning
This hip-hop infused song testifies that while God's timing may not match ours, He is never late and always acts perfectly. It echoes the promise in 2 Peter 3 that the Lord is not slow in keeping His promises, but operates on His sovereign timeline.
