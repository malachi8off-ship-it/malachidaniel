Tera Pyaar
Bridge Music
meaning
"Tera Pyaar," a worship song by Bridge Music, is performed in Hindi and centers on the theme of God’s unconditional love and the believer’s heartfelt response of devotion and surrender. The lyrics echo biblical passages that highlight God’s steadfast love—such as Psalm 103’s declaration of mercy and John 3:16’s promise of eternal life—while inviting listeners to experience and proclaim that love in their daily walk. By weaving scriptural truths with contemporary worship language, the song offers a concise, theologically grounded meditation on the nature and impact of divine love.
