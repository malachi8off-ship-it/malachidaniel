AMAZING!
ELEVATION RHYTHM
meaning
"AMAZING!" by Elevation Rhythm is a contemporary worship track that celebrates the awe‑inspiring nature of God’s love, grace, and faithfulness, inviting listeners to respond with heartfelt worship. The song draws on biblical themes such as the Psalmist’s declarations of God’s mercy and steadfast love (e.g., Psalm 103:8‑12) and the New Testament affirmation of God’s love for humanity (John 3:16), framing these truths as “amazing” and transformative. By weaving these scriptural references into its chorus and verses, the track encourages believers to recognize and proclaim the extraordinary character of God in their lives.
