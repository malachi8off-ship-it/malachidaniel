I Know A Name
Brandon Lake
meaning
"I Know a Name" by Brandon Lake is a contemporary worship song that centers on the intimate recognition of Jesus’ name as a source of power, redemption, and personal identity. The lyrics draw from biblical themes such as the authority of Christ’s name in passages like Philippians 2:9‑11 and the promise of His presence in John 14:13‑14, encouraging believers to trust in His transformative grace. The song’s message emphasizes that knowing and proclaiming Jesus’ name brings assurance, healing, and a deeper relationship with God.
