The Blessing
Elevation Worship
meaning
This song is a declaration of God's favor and peace over the listener and their future generations. The lyrics are directly based on the Aaronic Blessing found in Numbers 6:24-26, which says, 'The Lord bless you and keep you; the Lord make his face to shine upon you and be gracious'.
