You Say
Lauren Daigle
meaning
Released by contemporary Christian artist Lauren Daigle in 2018, the worship song "You Say" focuses on themes of identity, self-worth, and finding strength in God rather than worldly standards. Its lyrics center on overcoming feelings of inadequacy and insecurity by embracing how God defines a person as loved, strong, and chosen. The song's message is deeply rooted in biblical concepts of grace and divine affirmation, echoing passages such as 2 Corinthians 12:9 ("My grace is sufficient for you, for my power is made perfect in weakness") and Psalm 139, which emphasizes being fearfully and wonderfully made.
