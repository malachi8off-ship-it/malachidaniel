Eh Prabhu Yeshu
Sheldon Bangera
meaning
Sung in Hindi, this worship song is a passionate plea for Jesus to intervene, guide, and manifest His glory. It echoes the desperate prayers of the Psalms asking the Lord to make His presence known and felt.
