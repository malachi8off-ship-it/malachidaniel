Seasons (Live) - Hillsong Worship
Hillsong Worship
meaning
This contemplative song explores the patience required to trust God's timing through the metaphorical winters and springs of life. It is deeply rooted in Ecclesiastes 3, acknowledging that there is a time and season for every activity under heaven.
