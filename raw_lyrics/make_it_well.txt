Make It Well
MercyMe
meaning
"Make It Well" by MercyMe is a contemporary Christian worship song that explores themes of human brokenness, surrender, and the restorative power of faith. The track's core meaning centers on acknowledging personal flaws and struggles while seeking divine intervention for spiritual and emotional healing. Biblically, the song draws inspiration from accounts of miraculous physical and spiritual restoration in the Gospels, particularly the persistent faith of individuals seeking Jesus for wholeness.
