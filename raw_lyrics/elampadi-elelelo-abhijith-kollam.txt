Elampadi Elelelo
Abhijith Kollam
meaning
This track is performed in Malayalam and is styled as a traditional boat song (Vanchippattu), adapted for Christian celebrations and a festive, rustic atmosphere. The core meaning revolves around community rejoicing, shared labor, and local cultural harmony under divine grace. It mirrors the spirit of Psalm 133:1, celebrating the goodness and pleasantness of God's people dwelling together in unity.
