Haath Uthakar Gaunga (Live)
Bridge Music
meaning
"Haath Uthakar Gaunga (Live)" by Bridge Music is a contemporary Christian worship song sung primarily in Hinglish, translating to "I will sing with my hands raised." It conveys a worshipper's resolute commitment to ceaselessly praise God through all life's circumstances, declaring an unwavering devotion and surrender. The act of lifting hands in worship reflects biblical postures of adoration, supplication, and blessing, as seen in Psalms and other New Testament passages.
