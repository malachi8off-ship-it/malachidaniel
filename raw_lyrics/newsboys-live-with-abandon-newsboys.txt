Newsboys - Live With Abandon
Newsboys
meaning
This song is a commitment to stop living a safe, comfortable life and instead wholly dedicate oneself to God's radical call. It reflects Jesus' challenge in Mark 8 that whoever wants to save their life will lose it, but whoever loses their life for Him will save it.
