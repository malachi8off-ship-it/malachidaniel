Jesus Changed My Life
Katy Nichole
meaning
This song is a personal, joyful testimony of the radical transformation and freedom experienced after encountering Christ. It reflects the biblical truth of 2 Corinthians 5:17, that anyone in Christ is a new creation; the old has gone, the new is here.
