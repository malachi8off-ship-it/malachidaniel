Magnificent - Hillsong Worship
Hillsong Worship
meaning
This majestic worship song elevates the name and nature of Jesus, recognizing His glorious sacrifice and exalted position. It aligns with the Christological hymns of the New Testament, praising the unmatched splendor of the Savior.
