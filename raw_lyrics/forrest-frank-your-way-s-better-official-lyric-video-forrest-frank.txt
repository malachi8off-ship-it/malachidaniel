Forrest Frank
meaning
"Forrest Frank’s worship anthem “Your Way’s Better” encourages believers to trust in God’s guidance rather than their own plans, emphasizing that His path leads to peace and purpose. The lyrics echo biblical themes found in Proverbs 3:5‑6—faithfully following the Lord—and Psalm 23’s assurance of God’s steadfast care, inviting listeners to surrender personal agendas to divine wisdom. Through its uplifting melody and scriptural allusions, the song serves as a contemporary reminder that God’s direction ultimately brings fulfillment and safety."
