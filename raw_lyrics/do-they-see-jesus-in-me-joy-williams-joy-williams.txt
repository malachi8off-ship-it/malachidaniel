Do They See Jesus In Me?
Joy Williams, Joy Williams
meaning
This song is a reflective prayer asking if one's daily life and actions accurately reflect the character of Christ to others. It is rooted in the biblical call of Matthew 5 to let your light shine before others so they may glorify God.
