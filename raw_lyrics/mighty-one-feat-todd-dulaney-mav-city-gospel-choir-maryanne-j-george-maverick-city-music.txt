Mighty One (feat. Todd Dulaney, Mav City Gospel Choir & Maryanne J. George)
Maverick City Music
meaning
This powerful gospel anthem declares God's supreme power, unmatched strength, and victory over every enemy. It draws upon the many Old Testament titles for God, such as the Mighty One of Israel, praising His omnipotence.
