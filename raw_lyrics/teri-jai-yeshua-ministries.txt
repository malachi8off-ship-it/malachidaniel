Teri Jai
Yeshua Ministries
meaning
Sung in Hindi, this triumphant song declares victory and praise to God, celebrating His ultimate conquest over sin and death. It reflects the eschatological victory described in 1 Corinthians 15, where death is swallowed up in victory through Christ.
