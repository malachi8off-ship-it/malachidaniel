Matt Redman - 10,000 Reasons (Bless the Lord)
Matt Redman
meaning
This iconic worship song lists the endless reasons to praise God, from sunrise to the end of one's life. It is fundamentally anchored in Psalm 103, commanding the soul to bless the Lord and remember all His goodness.
