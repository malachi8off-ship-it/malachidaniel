Cathedrals Of The Nelder Grove
Benjamin William Hastings
meaning
This acoustic piece finds the majesty of God in the beauty of nature rather than man-made religious structures. It reflects the biblical idea from Romans 1 that God's invisible qualities are clearly seen in what has been made.
