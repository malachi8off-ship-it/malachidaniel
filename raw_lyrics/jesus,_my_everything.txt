Jesus, My Everything
William McDowell
meaning
"Jesus, My Everything" by William McDowell is a contemporary Christian worship song that centers on the theme of absolute devotion and total surrender to God as the ultimate source of spiritual fulfillment and sustenance. Rooted in biblical concepts of Jesus being the "all in all" and the provider of living water—echoing passages such as Colossians 3:11 and John 4:14—the lyrics express a believer's deep reliance on Christ above all earthly desires. Through its soulful, corporate worship style, the song serves as a meditative declaration that God alone is worthy of praise and capable of meeting every existential and spiritual need.
