Tumsa
Yeshua Ministries
meaning
Sung in Hindi, this song declares that there is absolutely no one like Jesus in love, power, and majesty. It reflects the biblical declarations in Isaiah and the Psalms that God is utterly unique and unmatched by any earthly idol or power.
