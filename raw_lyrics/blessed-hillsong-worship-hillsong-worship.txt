Blessed - Hillsong Worship
Hillsong Worship
meaning
This song rejoices in the spiritual blessings and peace given to those who walk closely with God. It echoes the Beatitudes in Matthew 5 and Psalm 1, describing the prosperous and joyful state of the righteous.
