O Come to the Altar
Elevation Worship
meaning
This song is an invitation for those struggling and burdened by sin to seek forgiveness and renewal through Jesus Christ. It emphasizes the Father's open arms and encourages listeners to leave behind their regrets and mistakes by turning to the altar.
