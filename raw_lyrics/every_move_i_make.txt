Every Move I Make
Christ Music Kids
meaning
"Every Move I Make" by Christ Music Kids is an energetic Christian worship song centered on themes of joyful praise, divine guidance, and total dependence on God. The lyrics draw biblical inspiration from passages like Acts 17:28 ("In him we live and move and have our being") and Psalm 121, expressing a believer's commitment to honoring God with their entire life, breath, and strength. Through its upbeat tempo and repetitive, easy-to-learn phrases, the song serves as a child-friendly expression of worship that celebrates a dynamic, everyday relationship with Jesus Christ.
