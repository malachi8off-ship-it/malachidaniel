Forrest Frank - LIFE IS GOOD (Official Audio)

meaning
This track celebrates the abundant life that Jesus provides, emphasizing gratitude for God's blessings. It is rooted in Jesus' statement in John 10:10 that He came so that His sheep may have life and have it to the full.
