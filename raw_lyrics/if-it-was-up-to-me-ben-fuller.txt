If It Was Up To Me
Ben Fuller
meaning
"If It Was Up to Me" by Ben Fuller is a contemporary worship song that encourages believers to surrender their personal desires to God’s sovereign will, emphasizing that His purpose is paramount over individual plans. Drawing from biblical themes such as Isaiah 55:8‑9 and Romans 12:1‑2, the song invites listeners to trust in God’s guidance and submit to His will, reflecting the Christian call to obedience and humility.
