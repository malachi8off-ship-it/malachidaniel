Been So Good
Elevation Worship
meaning
"Been So Good" by Elevation Worship is a contemporary Christian song that celebrates God's consistent faithfulness, mercy, and goodness throughout the believer's life. Drawing heavily on biblical themes of divine remembrance and thanksgiving—echoing scriptures such as Psalm 103:2, which encourages believers to "forget not all his benefits"—the track serves as a corporate expression of gratitude and testimony. Through its uplifting melody and congregational arrangement, the song directs worshippers to reflect on past grace as a foundation for enduring trust in God.
