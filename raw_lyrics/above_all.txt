Above All
Michael W. Smith
meaning
Released in 2002 by Christian artist Michael W. Smith, the worship song "Above All" reflects on the crucifixion of Jesus Christ and His supreme position above all earthly powers, creation, and human understanding. The lyrics draw heavily from biblical themes found in Philippians 2:9–11, which describe God exalting Jesus above every name, as well as Isaiah 53:3, which portrays Him as "despised and rejected by mankind" and likened to a crucified flower "trampled on the ground." Through its reverent melody and poignant imagery, the song emphasizes the sacrificial nature of Christ's death and resurrection while exalting His enduring divinity and grace.
