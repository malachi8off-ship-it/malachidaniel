All Is Well [Feat. Joy Williams]
Avi Kaplan feat. Joy Williams
meaning
This peaceful song offers reassurance that despite life's turmoil, everything rests securely in God's hands. It draws upon the biblical promise of the peace that surpasses all understanding found in Philippians 4.
