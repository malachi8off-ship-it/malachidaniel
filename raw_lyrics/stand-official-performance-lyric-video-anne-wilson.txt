Stand (Official Performance Lyric Video)
Anne Wilson
meaning
This song encourages believers to plant their feet firmly on the truth of God's Word when the world around them is shifting. It draws on the imagery of Ephesians 6, urging Christians to put on the armor of God so they can stand their ground.
