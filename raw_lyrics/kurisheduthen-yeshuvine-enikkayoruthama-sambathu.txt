Kurisheduthen Yeshuvine-Enikkayoruthama Sambathu
Rex Media House
meaning
"Kurisheduthen Yeshuvine-Enikkayoruthama Sambathu" is a Malayalam Christian worship song produced by Rex Media House that reflects upon the profound spiritual wealth found in taking up the cross and following Jesus. Drawing from biblical themes of self-denial and discipleship found in the Gospels—such as Luke 9:23—the lyrics center on surrendering worldly pursuits to gain the ultimate, eternal treasure of Christ's fellowship. Sung entirely in Malayalam, the devotional track encourages believers to find their true identity and utmost riches in embracing their daily crosses for the sake of the Savior.
