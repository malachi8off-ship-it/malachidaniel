Zach Williams - Friend in High Places (Visualizer)

meaning
This country-rock song joyfully testifies that despite a messy past, the singer has the ultimate advocate and friend in Jesus. It reflects the biblical truth of 1 John 2:1, that believers have an advocate with the Father—Jesus Christ, the Righteous One.
