Perfectly
Micah Tyler
meaning
"Perfectly" by Micah Tyler is a Christian worship song that conveys the message of being perfectly loved and accepted by God, despite one's flaws and imperfections. The song's core meaning is rooted in the biblical concept of God's unconditional love and acceptance, as seen in 1 John 3:1, where it is written, "See what great love the Father has lavished on us, that we should be called children of God!" The song's lyrics reflect this idea, emphasizing the freedom and joy that comes from knowing one is perfectly loved by God.
