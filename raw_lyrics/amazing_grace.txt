Amazing Grace
Gravel & Grace
meaning
"Amazing Grace" by Gravel & Grace is a contemporary rendition of the classic Christian hymn, centered on the themes of divine redemption, forgiveness, and unearned mercy. The song reflects the foundational New Testament theology—particularly found in Ephesians 2:8–9—that salvation is a free gift from God through faith, rather than the result of human works. Musically and lyrically, the track emphasizes the transformative power of grace in lifting an individual out of spiritual lostness and despair.
