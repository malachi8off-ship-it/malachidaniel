Better Than I
Joy Williams, Joy Williams
meaning
This song is an acknowledgment of God's sovereign wisdom and a surrender of personal control. It reflects the biblical wisdom of Proverbs 3:5-6, trusting in the Lord with all one's heart rather than one's own understanding.
