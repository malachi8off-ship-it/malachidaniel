Katy Nichole - "When I Fall" (Official Music Video)
Katy Nichole
meaning
This song expresses the vulnerability of human weakness and the comforting certainty that God's arms are there to catch us. It resonates with Psalm 37:24, which states that though the righteous stumble, they will not fall, for the Lord upholds them with His hand.
