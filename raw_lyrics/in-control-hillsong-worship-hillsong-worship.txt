In Control - Hillsong Worship
Hillsong Worship
meaning
This worship song is a declaration of trust in God's absolute sovereignty, even when the world feels chaotic. It brings comfort through the biblical truth found in Psalm 46 that God is our refuge and strength, an ever-present help in trouble.
