Aathmavil Niranjavarayi Anayunnitha
Abhijith Kollam
meaning
This Malayalam Christian worship song centers on believers gathering together while being filled with the Holy Spirit. It highlights the transformation, joy, and unity that the Divine Spirit brings into the community of faith during corporate worship. This aligns with Ephesians 5:18-19, which instructs believers to be filled with the Spirit and to sing spiritual songs to one another.
