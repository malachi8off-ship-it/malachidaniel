OKAY!
Forrest Frank
meaning
Released in 2024, the Christian pop song "OKAY!" by Forrest Frank encourages listeners to maintain faith and peace during difficult times by trusting in God's ultimate control. The song's core message centers on the assurance that no matter the earthly circumstances, believers can ultimately feel secure because their lives are anchored in divine providence. This theme draws biblical context from passages like Romans 8:28, which assures believers that God works all things together for good, as well as Jesus' teachings in the Sermon on the Mount (Matthew 6:25-34) about letting go of anxiety and trusting in God's provision.
