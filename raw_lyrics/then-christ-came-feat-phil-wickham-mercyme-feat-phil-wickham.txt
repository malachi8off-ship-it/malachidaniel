Then Christ Came (feat. Phil Wickham)
MercyMe feat. Phil Wickham
meaning
This collaborative track celebrates the radical, night-and-day transformation that occurs when Jesus enters a hopeless situation. It reflects the biblical concept found in Ephesians 2, moving from being dead in sin to being made alive in Christ.
