No Sweeter Name
Kari Jobe
meaning
This worship song is an intimate expression of adoration for the name of Jesus, finding ultimate comfort and peace in Him. It reflects the beauty of Song of Solomon 1:3, where the name of the beloved is described as a pouring perfume.
