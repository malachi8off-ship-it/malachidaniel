Brandon Lake - COUNTRY PSALM (Music Video)
Brandon Lake
meaning
This acoustic song is a simple, earnest prayer of gratitude for God's everyday provisions and creation. It channels the rustic, pastoral imagery of the Psalms, particularly Psalm 23's depiction of the Lord as a Shepherd.
