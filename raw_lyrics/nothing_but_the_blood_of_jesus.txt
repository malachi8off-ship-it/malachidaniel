Nothing But The Blood Of Jesus
Carrie Underwood
meaning
Carrie Underwood’s contemporary recording of the traditional Christian hymn "Nothing But the Blood of Jesus" celebrates the central Christian doctrine of atonement and redemption through the crucifixion of Jesus Christ. The song's core message emphasizes that human efforts and good works are insufficient for spiritual salvation, asserting instead that only the shed blood of Jesus can cleanse humanity from sin and restore a relationship with God. This theme directly reflects New Testament theology, drawing upon foundational biblical passages such as Hebrews 9:22, which states that "without shedding of blood there is no forgiveness," and 1 John 1:7, which affirms that the blood of Jesus cleanses believers from all sin.
