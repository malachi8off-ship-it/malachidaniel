Charity Gayle
meaning
Charity Gayle's "I Speak Jesus" is a fervent worship anthem declaring the supreme power and authority of Jesus' name over all life's struggles, including fear, sickness, and anxiety. Rooted in biblical teachings that emphasize the redemptive and healing efficacy found in proclaiming Jesus, the song invites believers to find comfort, deliverance, and hope by consistently speaking and trusting in His name, reflecting scriptures that highlight His name above all others.
