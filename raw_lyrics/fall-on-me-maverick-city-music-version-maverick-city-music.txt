Fall On Me (Maverick City Music Version)
Maverick City Music
meaning
This worship piece is a desperate cry for the Holy Spirit to fall fresh on the believer, bringing renewal and power. It connects to the biblical theme of God pouring water on thirsty land, as promised in Isaiah 44:3.
