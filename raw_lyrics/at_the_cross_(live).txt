At the Cross (Live)
Hillsong Worship
meaning
"At the Cross (Live)" by Hillsong Worship is an English worship song that profoundly expresses gratitude for Jesus Christ's redemptive sacrifice on the cross. It centers on the core Christian belief that through Christ's death, believers receive forgiveness, freedom, and new life, deeply rooted in biblical passages describing the atonement for sins (e.g., John 3:16, Romans 5:8). The song invites worshippers to reflect on God's immense love and the transformative power found at the foot of the cross.
