Mighty Name Of Jesus (Live)
Hope Darst
meaning
"Mighty Name Of Jesus (Live)" by Hope Darst is a contemporary worship anthem that celebrates the authority and transformative power of Jesus’ name, urging believers to trust in its victorious strength. The lyrics draw on biblical passages such as Revelation 12:11, Psalm 115:3, and Philippians 2:9‑11, emphasizing that the name of Jesus is above all names and can bring deliverance, healing, and renewal. By combining a soaring musical arrangement with scriptural references, the song invites worshippers to proclaim and experience the mighty name of Christ in both personal devotion and corporate praise.
