Bridge Music
meaning
Sung in Hindi, this song is a beautiful testament to God's absolute, unwavering faithfulness throughout all circumstances. It is deeply rooted in 2 Timothy 2:13, which states that even if we are faithless, He remains faithful, for He cannot disown Himself.
