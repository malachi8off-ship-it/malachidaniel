Angu Dhoore Kalvariyil
Abhijith Kollam
meaning
Sung in Malayalam, this solemn Lenten and Holy Week devotional song reflects on the raw suffering of Jesus Christ on Mount Calvary. The lyrics express deep grief and gratitude as the narrator observes Christ bearing human sins through crucifixion. It captures the biblical narrative of Isaiah 53:5, which speaks of Christ being wounded for human transgressions.
