CeCe Winans - Holy Forever (Official Music Video)
CeCe Winans
meaning
"CeCe Winans - Holy Forever (Official Music Video)" is a Christian worship song by CeCe Winans, where she expresses her unwavering devotion to God, declaring that He is forever holy and worthy of praise. The song's core meaning revolves around the biblical concept of God's holiness and sovereignty, as mentioned in Psalm 99:9, where it says, "Exalt the Lord our God and worship at his holy mountain; for the Lord our God is holy." The song's lyrics convey a sense of reverence and awe for God's majesty, reflecting the Christian doctrine that God is eternal, all-powerful, and worthy of worship.
