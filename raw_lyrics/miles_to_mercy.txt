Miles to Mercy
Gravel & Grace
meaning
"Miles to Mercy" by Gravel & Grace is a Christian worship song that explores the themes of human brokenness, the long journey toward spiritual redemption, and the relentless pursuit of God's grace. Rooted in biblical concepts of repentance and the parable of the Prodigal Son, the lyrics reflect the sinner's acknowledgment of distance from God and the transformative power of unearned divine forgiveness. Through its contemplative tone, the song emphasizes that no matter how far one has strayed, God’s mercy bridges the gap to bring the believer home.
