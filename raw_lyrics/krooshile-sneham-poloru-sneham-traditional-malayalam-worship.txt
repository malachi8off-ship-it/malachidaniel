Krooshile Sneham Poloru Sneham
Traditional Malayalam Worship
meaning
This worship song profoundly extols the unparalleled love of God revealed through Christ's sacrifice on the Cross, which conquered darkness and eternal death (Romans 6:23). It highlights the transformative power of this love, granting believers adoption into God's family as sons and daughters (Romans 8:15), prompting a heartfelt response of continuous gratitude and worship to God as "Abba." The core biblical message centers on the unique redemptive love of Christ and the resultant call to praise.
