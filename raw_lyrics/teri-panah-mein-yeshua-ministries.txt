Teri Panah Mein
Yeshua Ministries
meaning
Sung in Hindi, this comforting song speaks of finding ultimate safety, refuge, and peace under the shelter of God's wings. It is a beautiful lyrical adaptation of Psalm 91, dwelling in the secret place of the Most High.
