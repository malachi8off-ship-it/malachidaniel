I Can Only Imagine
MercyMe
meaning
Released in 2001 by the contemporary Christian band MercyMe, "I Can Only Imagine" is a widely popular worship song that explores a believer's speculative thoughts on what it will be like to stand before Jesus Christ in heaven. Written by lead singer Bart Millard following the death of his father, the lyrics grapple with the overwhelming emotions of awe, reverence, and surrender depicted in biblical passages like 1 John 3:2, which states that when Christ appears, believers "shall be like him, for we shall see him as he is." Ultimately, the song serves as a meditation on the Christian hope of the afterlife, eternal worship, and the ultimate meeting between humanity and the divine.
