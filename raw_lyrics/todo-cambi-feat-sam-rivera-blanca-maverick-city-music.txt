Todo Cambió (feat. Sam Rivera & Blanca)
Maverick City Music
meaning
Sung in Spanish, this song testifies to the radical transformation and paradigm shift that occurs when a person encounters Jesus. It embodies the truth of 2 Corinthians 5:17, that in Christ, everything old has gone and everything has become new.
