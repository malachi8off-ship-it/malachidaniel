Sinking - Jars of Clay
Jars of Clay
meaning
This song explores the feeling of being overwhelmed by the world and the desperate need for Jesus to reach down and save. It mirrors the biblical account of Peter walking on the water in Matthew 14, crying out 'Lord, save me!' as he began to sink.
