Sadaa Raja (Live Version) (feat. Joseph Raj Allam
Nations of Worship
meaning
Sung in Hindi, this live worship anthem praises Jesus as the eternal, everlasting King who reigns forever. It echoes the promise given to Mary in Luke 1, that Christ will reign over Jacob’s descendants forever, and His kingdom will never end.
