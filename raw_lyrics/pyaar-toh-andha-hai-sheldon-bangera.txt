Pyaar Toh Andha Hai
Sheldon Bangera
meaning
Sung in Hindi, this song playfully but profoundly reflects on how God's love overlooks our faults and loves us unconditionally. It echoes the biblical truth that love covers over a multitude of sins, as stated in 1 Peter 4:8.
