Worthy is the lamb
Don Moen
meaning
"Worthy Is the Lamb" by Don Moen is a contemporary Christian worship song that exalts Jesus Christ as the supreme, sacrificial savior who redeemed humanity through His death and resurrection. Lyrically and conceptually, the song is deeply rooted in the biblical imagery of the Book of Revelation—specifically Revelation 5:12, which depicts heavenly beings proclaiming that the slaughtered Lamb is worthy to receive power, wealth, wisdom, strength, honor, glory, and praise. Through its reverent melody and corporate worship style, the song invites believers to join in eternal praise, reflecting themes of gratitude for divine grace, redemption, and the supreme lordship of Christ.
