Holding On
Jon Reddick
meaning
"Holding On" by Jon Reddick is a contemporary Christian worship song that encourages believers to trust in God's steadfast love and faithfulness amid life's trials, emphasizing the promise that He will never abandon His people. The lyrics draw on biblical themes such as Psalm 23’s assurance of God’s guidance and protection, and Hebrews 6:19’s metaphor of hope as a “sure and steadfast anchor.” Through its uplifting melody and heartfelt exhortation, the song invites listeners to cling to God’s promises and remain steadfast in faith.
