I Surrender - Hillsong Worship
Hillsong Worship
meaning
This deeply emotional worship song is a cry to know God more and a yielding of one's own desires to the Holy Spirit. It embodies the biblical call in Romans 12:1 to offer one's body as a living sacrifice, holy and pleasing to God.
