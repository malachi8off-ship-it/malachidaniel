In The Fire - Josiah Queen (Official Lyric Video)
Josiah Queen
meaning
This song declares that God's presence is most deeply felt during the most difficult, fiery trials of life. It draws directly from the story of Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego in Daniel 3, where a fourth man appears in the fiery furnace.
