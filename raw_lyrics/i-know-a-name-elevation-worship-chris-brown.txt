I Know A Name
Elevation Worship, Chris Brown
meaning
"I Know A Name" is a worship anthem that celebrates the personal intimacy believers have with Jesus, emphasizing that He knows each individual by name. The lyrics draw from biblical passages such as John 1:12, Philippians 2:9‑11, and Revelation 3:20, underscoring the believer’s identity in Christ and the assurance of His presence. The song invites worshippers to respond with gratitude and devotion, highlighting the transformative power of knowing God personally.
