Casting Crowns - Great Are You Lord (Official Live Performance)
Casting Crowns
meaning
This worship anthem acknowledges God as the giver of breath and life, prompting a response of wholehearted praise. It is rooted in Genesis 2 and Ezekiel 37, where the breath of God brings life to the dead.
