Revelation Song
Kari Jobe
meaning
This breathtaking worship song is a direct lyrical adaptation of the heavenly throne room vision. It pulls heavily from Revelation 4 and 5, where the angels and elders sing 'Holy, Holy, Holy' to the Lord God Almighty.
