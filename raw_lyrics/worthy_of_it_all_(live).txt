Worthy Of It All (Live)
CeCe Winans
meaning
"Worthy Of It All (Live)" by CeCe Winans is a powerful contemporary worship anthem that exalts God as the ultimate recipient of all glory, honor, and praise. Rooted heavily in the apocalyptic imagery of the Book of Revelation—specifically echoing the heavenly scenes where elders and living creatures bow before the throne to worship the Lamb—the song's core message emphasizes perpetual adoration and the belief that humanity and creation exist solely to magnify the divine holiness of God.
