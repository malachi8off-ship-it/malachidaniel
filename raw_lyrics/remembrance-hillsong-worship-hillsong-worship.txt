Remembrance - Hillsong Worship
Hillsong Worship
meaning
This reverent song focuses on the sacrament of communion, remembering the physical sacrifice of Christ's body and blood on the cross. It is a direct musical observance of Jesus' command in Luke 22 to 'do this in remembrance of me.'
