Tu Hai Mahan
Yeshua Ministries
meaning
Sung in Hindi, this worship song is a simple, powerful declaration of God's immense greatness and sovereignty. It reflects the universal call of the Psalms to ascribe to the Lord the glory due His name because of His mighty acts.
