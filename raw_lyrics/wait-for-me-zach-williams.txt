Wait For Me
Zach Williams
meaning
"Wait For Me" by Zach Williams is a contemporary Christian worship song centered on the themes of patience, trust in God's timing, and surrender during seasons of personal struggle and waiting. Drawing inspiration from biblical passages that encourage believers to lean not on their own understanding and to wait upon the Lord for renewal, the song serves as a reminder to rely on divine strength rather than rushing ahead of God's plan. Through its reflective lyrics and soulful delivery, it encourages listeners to find peace and perseverance while awaiting divine guidance.
