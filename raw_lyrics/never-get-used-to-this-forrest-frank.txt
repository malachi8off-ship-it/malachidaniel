NEVER GET USED TO THIS
Forrest Frank
meaning
"Never Get Used to This" by Forrest Frank is a contemporary worship song that emphasizes the perpetual renewal of God’s love and grace, encouraging listeners to remain in awe of His constant faithfulness. The lyrics draw on biblical themes of God’s unchanging nature and the promise of new mercies, echoing passages such as Psalm 103:8 and Isaiah 43:18‑19, and invite worshippers to continually experience the fresh outpouring of divine love. The song is performed in English, reflecting Forrest Frank’s American worship style.
