Lauren Daigle - Turn Your Eyes (Audio)
Lauren Daigle
meaning
This song is a modern reimagining of a classic hymn, urging listeners to focus their gaze on Jesus rather than earthly struggles. It reflects the biblical command in Hebrews 12 to fix our eyes on Jesus, the pioneer and perfecter of faith.
