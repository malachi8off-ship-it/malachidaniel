That's The Thing About Praise
Benjamin William Hastings
meaning
This song reflects on the paradoxical power of praising God in the middle of pain, noting how it shifts our perspective. It draws on the biblical concept of the 'sacrifice of praise' from Hebrews 13, worshipping when it defies logic.
