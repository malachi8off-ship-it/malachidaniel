Jai Jai Naam (Hail Jesus' Name)
Sheldon Bangera
meaning
Sung in Hindi, this anthem is a celebration of the victory and salvation found solely in the name of Jesus Christ. It echoes the biblical truth that there is no other name under heaven given to mankind by which we must be saved.
