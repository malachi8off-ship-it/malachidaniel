Jai Jai Naam (Hail Jesus' Name)
Sheldon Bangera
meaning
Sheldon Bangera’s worship anthem “Jai Jai Naam (Hail Jesus’ Name)” is performed in Hindi and invites listeners to exalt the name of Jesus with fervent adoration. The song’s core message centers on the supremacy and transformative power of Jesus’ name, urging believers to proclaim it as a source of salvation and divine authority. Biblically, it echoes passages such as Psalm 103:1 (“Praise the LORD, O my soul”), John 17:1 (“I pray for you, Father”), and 1 John 5:13, which affirm that the name of Jesus holds the power to bring believers into the presence of God.
