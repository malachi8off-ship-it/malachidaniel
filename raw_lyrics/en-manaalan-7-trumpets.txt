En Manaalan
7 Trumpets
meaning
Sung in Malayalam, this Christian worship song explores the intimate spiritual relationship between the believer and Christ, who is portrayed as the ultimate bridegroom. The theme focuses on finding comfort, peace, and eternal hope in the Savior despite earthly tribulations and sorrow. The imagery draws directly from the biblical theology of Christ as the Bridegroom found in Matthew 25 and the Book of Revelation.
