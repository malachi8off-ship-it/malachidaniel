Reckless Love
Cory Asbury
meaning
"Reckless Love," a contemporary worship song by Cory Asbury, underscores the relentless, sacrificial nature of God's love, drawing on biblical themes such as the prodigal son and the overarching narrative of divine grace. The lyrics emphasize that God’s love is unmerited, persistent, and actively seeks to draw believers back to Him, echoing passages like 1 John 4:8 and Psalm 103:8.
