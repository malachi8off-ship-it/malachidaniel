Zariya
Yeshua Ministries
meaning
Zariya, a worship song by Yeshua Ministries, is sung in Hindi and emphasizes that the only true way to God is through Jesus Christ, urging believers to trust in His grace and follow His teachings. Drawing on biblical passages such as John 14:6, Philippians 4:13, and Romans 10:9, the song encourages listeners to embrace faith, surrender to God’s will, and live a life of obedience and hope.
