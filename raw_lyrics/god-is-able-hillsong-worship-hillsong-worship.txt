God Is Able - Hillsong Worship
Hillsong Worship
meaning
This anthem builds faith by declaring that God can overcome any obstacle, defeat, or impossibility. It is firmly rooted in Ephesians 3:20, praising the God who is able to do immeasurably more than all we ask or imagine.
