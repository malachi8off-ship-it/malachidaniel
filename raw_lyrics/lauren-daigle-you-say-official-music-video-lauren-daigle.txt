meaning
"You Say" by Lauren Daigle is a Christian worship song that conveys a message of hope and reassurance, emphasizing the idea that God's love and acceptance are unconditional, despite one's past mistakes or current struggles. The song draws from biblical teachings, particularly Psalm 23 and Romans 8:1, which emphasize God's unwavering love and redemption. The song's lyrics, sung in English, express a personal and intimate conversation with God, highlighting the comfort and peace that comes from knowing one is loved and forgiven.
