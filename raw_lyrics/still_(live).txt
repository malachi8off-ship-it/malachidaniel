Still (Live)
Hillsong Worship
meaning
"Still (Live)" by Hillsong Worship is a worship song that encourages believers to find peace and rest in God's unwavering presence amidst life's challenges. Drawing deeply from themes of trust and sovereignty found in scriptures like Psalm 46:10 ("Be still, and know that I am God"), it reassures listeners that God is their refuge and strength. The song ultimately calls for a surrender to God's control, reminding us of His faithfulness through every storm and offering comfort in His constant presence.
