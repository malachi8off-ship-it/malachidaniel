What A God
SEU Worship, ONE HOUSE, Kenzie Walker, Chelsea Plank
meaning
This collaborative piece expresses awe and reverence toward God's immense majesty, limitless power, and miraculous interventions. It encourages corporate adoration by recounting God's character as a promise-keeper who performs the impossible. The biblical underpinnings are drawn from the praises of Psalm 86:10 and Ephesians 3:20, which exalt God for His wondrous deeds and infinite capability.
