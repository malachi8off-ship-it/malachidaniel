Breathe On It (Official Lyric Video)
Tauren Wells
meaning
Tauren Wells' 'Breathe On It' is an English Christian worship song that serves as a fervent prayer for God's divine intervention and life-giving power to revitalize stagnant situations, bring hope, and resurrect what seems dead. Drawing heavily on biblical imagery like the breath of life given to Adam (Genesis 2:7) and the vision of dry bones in Ezekiel 37, the song implores the Holy Spirit to infuse new life and purpose into every area of life, emphasizing divine restoration.
