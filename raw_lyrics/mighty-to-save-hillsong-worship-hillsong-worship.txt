Mighty to Save - Hillsong Worship
Hillsong Worship
meaning
This classic worship song proclaims that Jesus is the author of salvation, capable of conquering the grave and redeeming any life. It reflects the prophecy in Zephaniah 3:17 that the Lord is in our midst, a mighty one who will save.
