Teri Aaradhana Ho
Bridge Music
meaning
"Teri Aaradhana Ho" is a worship hymn by Bridge Music that celebrates the believer’s devotion to God, urging surrender and adoration in spirit and truth. The lyrics draw from biblical themes of worship found in Psalms and Revelation, encouraging listeners to worship God wholeheartedly and faithfully. The song is performed in Hindi, making it accessible to Hindi‑speaking congregations.
