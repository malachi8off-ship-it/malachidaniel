michael w. smith - don't give up
Michael W. Smith
meaning
This track is an encouraging anthem urging listeners to hold onto hope and persevere through dark and difficult times. It echoes the biblical exhortation in Galatians 6:9 to not grow weary in doing good, for at the proper time we will reap a harvest.
