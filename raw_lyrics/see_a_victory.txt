See A Victory
Elevation Worship
meaning
This song emphasizes the belief that God fights for His people, assuring them of victory over their struggles through faith in Jesus Christ. The lyrics highlight that God proactively ordains events to take place while simultaneously using them for our good and His glory, referencing Genesis 50:20.
