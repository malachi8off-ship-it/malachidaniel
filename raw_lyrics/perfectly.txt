Perfectly
Micah Tyler
meaning
"Perfectly" by Micah Tyler is a contemporary Christian worship song that explores the theme of human inadequacy contrasted with divine grace, assuring listeners that they are completely loved and accepted by God despite their flaws. Rooted in biblical concepts of unconditional love and adoption—such as Romans 5:8, which states that God demonstrates His love for us in that while we were still sinners, Christ died for us—the song encourages believers to release the pressure of self-perfection and instead rest in the finished work of Jesus.
