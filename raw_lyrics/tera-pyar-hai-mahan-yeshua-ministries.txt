Tera Pyar Hai Mahan
Yeshua Ministries
meaning
Sung in Hindi, this worship anthem is an exaltation of the greatness, depth, and unmatched nature of God's love. It echoes the Apostle Paul's prayer in Ephesians 3, marveling at a love that surpasses all knowledge.
