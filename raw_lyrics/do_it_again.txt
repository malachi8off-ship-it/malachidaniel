Do It Again
Elevation Worship
meaning
Elevation Worship's 'Do It Again' is a worship song that centers on the core belief in God's unwavering faithfulness. It encourages listeners to recall past instances of divine intervention and provision, drawing on biblical truths that God's character is unchanging, to confidently trust He will act again in current and future challenges. The song posits that God's historical reliability guarantees His future faithfulness, asserting He "never fails" and "won't start now."
