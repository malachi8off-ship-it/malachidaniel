Stand My Ground
Zach Williams
meaning
This southern rock song is a defiant commitment to remain steadfast in faith despite opposition or cultural pressure. It reflects the biblical command in 1 Corinthians 16:13 to stand firm in the faith, be courageous, and be strong.
