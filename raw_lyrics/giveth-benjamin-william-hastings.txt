Giveth
Benjamin William Hastings
meaning
This song wrestles with the complex reality of a God who provides blessings but also permits loss. It directly quotes Job 1:21, acknowledging that 'the Lord giveth and the Lord taketh away,' choosing to bless His name regardless.
