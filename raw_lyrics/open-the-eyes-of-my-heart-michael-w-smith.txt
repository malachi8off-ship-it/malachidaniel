Open The Eyes of My Heart
Michael W. Smith
meaning
This classic worship song is a passionate plea for spiritual illumination to see God's glory, power, and holiness clearly. It is a direct lyrical prayer drawn from Ephesians 1:18, asking that the eyes of the heart may be enlightened.
