CeCe Winans
meaning
CeCe Winans’ “Worthy Of It All” is a contemporary worship anthem that celebrates God’s sovereign grace and the believer’s complete reliance on Him, urging listeners to surrender all to the divine. The lyrics draw from biblical themes of redemption and worthiness, echoing passages such as Ephesians 2:8‑9 and Psalm 96:13, which affirm that humanity is made worthy only through Christ’s sacrifice. Musically, the track blends soulful gospel harmonies with a modern pop sensibility, making it a popular choice for both corporate worship settings and personal devotion.
