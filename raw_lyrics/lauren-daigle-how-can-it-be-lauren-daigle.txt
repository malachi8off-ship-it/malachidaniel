Lauren Daigle - How Can It Be
Lauren Daigle
meaning
This powerful worship song marvels at the scandalous grace of God that forgives deep sin and offers unconditional love. It reflects the biblical story of the woman caught in adultery in John 8, highlighting Christ's mercy over condemnation.
