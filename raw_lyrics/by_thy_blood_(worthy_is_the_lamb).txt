By Thy Blood (Worthy Is the Lamb)
CeCe Winans
meaning
"By Thy Blood (Worthy Is the Lamb)" by CeCe Winans is a contemporary Christian worship song centered on the redemptive power of Jesus Christ's sacrifice on the cross. Drawing heavy biblical context from the Book of Revelation—specifically the imagery of the Lamb who was slain to ransom people for God—the lyrics express profound gratitude and adoration for divine forgiveness. Ultimately, the song serves as an anthemic declaration of praise, celebrating the holiness and supreme worthiness of Jesus as depicted in heavenly worship.
