Oceans (Hillsong United Remix)
FOA Rockout
meaning
Oceans (Hillsong United Remix) by FOA Rockout is a contemporary worship track that reimagines the original Hillsong United anthem with a dynamic rock arrangement, emphasizing the call to trust in God’s guidance amid uncertainty. Drawing from the biblical narrative of Jesus walking on water (Matthew 14:22‑33) and the theme of stepping beyond one’s comfort zone, the song encourages listeners to surrender fear and embrace faith.
