Black Sheep
Ben Fuller
meaning
Ben Fuller’s worship song “Black Sheep” uses the biblical metaphor of the lost sheep to highlight God’s relentless pursuit and unconditional love for the outcast, echoing passages such as Matthew 18:12‑14 and Luke 15:4‑7. The track encourages listeners to recognize their own need for redemption and to seek God’s grace, framing the believer’s identity as a cherished child of God. Sung in English, the song invites reflection on the themes of divine grace, repentance, and the assurance that no one is truly lost in God’s sight.
