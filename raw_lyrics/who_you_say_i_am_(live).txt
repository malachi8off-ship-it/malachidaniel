Who You Say I Am (Live)
Hillsong Worship
meaning
"Who You Say I Am (Live)" is a Hillsong Worship anthem that celebrates the identity of believers as chosen, redeemed, and loved by God, emphasizing that their worth is defined by His love rather than human judgment. The lyrics draw from biblical passages such as Psalm 139, Romans 8, and Ephesians 1, underscoring that identity is rooted in Christ’s grace and purpose. The live recording captures the communal worship experience, reinforcing the message that believers are defined by God’s love and mission.
