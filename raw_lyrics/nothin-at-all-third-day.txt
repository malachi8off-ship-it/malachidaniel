Nothin' At All
Third Day
meaning
This rock track points out the futility of gaining worldly wealth and status if one's soul is lost in the process. It is a direct musical application of Jesus' warning in Matthew 16:26 about the ultimate worthlessness of gaining the whole world but losing your soul.
