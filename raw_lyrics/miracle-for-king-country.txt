meaning
"Miracle" by for KING & COUNTRY is a contemporary worship song that celebrates God's power to transform lives through divine intervention, urging listeners to trust in His ability to bring about change even when circumstances seem impossible. The lyrics reference biblical themes of faith, redemption, and the miraculous works of God as seen in passages such as Isaiah 43:19 and Matthew 19:26, encouraging believers to rely on His promise of new beginnings. With its upbeat pop‑rock instrumentation and heartfelt worship tone, the track has become a popular choice for congregational singing and personal devotion.
