God Is Good
Phil Wickham
meaning
This song is a simple yet profound declaration of God's inherent benevolence, seeing His goodness in both the cross and creation. It points to the foundational truth of Psalm 34:8, inviting listeners to 'taste and see that the Lord is good.'
