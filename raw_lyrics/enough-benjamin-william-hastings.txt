Enough!?
Benjamin William Hastings
meaning
This song wrestles with the sufficiency of Christ amidst a culture of constant striving and dissatisfaction. It points to the biblical truth in 2 Corinthians 12:9 that God's grace is entirely sufficient in our weakness.
