OKAY!
Forrest Frank
meaning
“OKAY!” by Forrest Frank is a contemporary worship anthem that encourages believers to surrender their anxieties and plans to God’s sovereign will, affirming that He is in control and that “everything is fine” when we trust Him. The song’s core message—letting go of human worry and embracing divine peace—echoes biblical themes such as Psalm 46’s declaration that God is our refuge and Isaiah 41:10’s promise that He will strengthen and uphold us. By weaving these scriptural truths into a simple, repetitive refrain, the track invites worshippers to experience the assurance that God’s presence makes all things “okay.”
