meaning
"Jesus Be The Name" by Elevation Worship is a Christian worship song that emphasizes the supremacy of Jesus Christ's name and the power it holds in believers' lives. The song's core meaning is centered around the biblical concept of Jesus being the name above every name, as stated in Philippians 2:9-10, where it says "that at the name of Jesus every knee should bow, in heaven and on earth and under the earth." This song is typically sung in English, and its message serves as a declaration of worship and surrender to Jesus' authority.
