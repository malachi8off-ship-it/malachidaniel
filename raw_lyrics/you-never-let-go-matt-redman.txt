You never let go
Matt Redman
meaning
This classic worship song provides assurance that even in the darkest valleys and fiercest storms, God's grip on us remains secure. It is heavily inspired by Psalm 23, finding comfort in the Shepherd's constant presence through the valley of the shadow of death.
