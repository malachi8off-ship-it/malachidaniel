Tell Me, How Is Heaven
May The Lord Studio
meaning
"Tell Me, How Is Heaven" by May The Lord Studio is a contemporary worship song that invites believers to contemplate the nature of the eternal kingdom, expressing a yearning to experience the joy and peace promised in Scripture. Drawing on biblical imagery from Revelation 21 and 2 Corinthians 5:1, the lyrics emphasize the hope of living in God’s presence forever and encourage worshipers to seek that reality in their present faith journey. The song’s heartfelt melody and reflective lyrics aim to deepen the listener’s understanding of heaven as described in Christian theology.
