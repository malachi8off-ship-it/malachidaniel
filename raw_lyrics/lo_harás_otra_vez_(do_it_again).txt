Lo Harás Otra Vez (Do It Again)
Elevation Worship
meaning
Elevation Worship's 'Lo Harás Otra Vez (Do It Again)' is a declaration of unwavering trust in God's consistent faithfulness. The song expresses confidence that just as God has demonstrated His power and provision in the past, He will continue to act on behalf of His people in present and future challenges. This core meaning draws heavily on biblical themes where God's unchanging character and past miracles serve as the foundation for believers' hope and perseverance during times of uncertainty.
