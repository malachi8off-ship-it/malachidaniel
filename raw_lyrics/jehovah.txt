Jehovah
Elevation Worship
meaning
"Jehovah" by Elevation Worship is a contemporary Christian worship song that celebrates the multifaceted nature and steadfast faithfulness of God as revealed through His various scriptural names. Drawing heavily from Old Testament passages where God is identified as a provider, healer, and refuge—such as Jehovah-Jireh and Jehovah-Rapha—the lyrics encourage believers to trust in His divine provision and sovereign power during times of trial. Ultimately, the song serves as an anthemic declaration of praise, grounding its message in the biblical assurance that God remains actively present and unchanging throughout human history.
