What a Beautiful Name (Live)
Hillsong Worship
meaning
"What a Beautiful Name (Live)" by Hillsong Worship is a powerful Christian worship song that exalts the supreme glory, power, and redemptive nature of Jesus Christ's name. It lyrically celebrates His divinity, triumph over sin and death, and unique identity as the world's Savior, drawing on biblical themes of His sovereignty and the saving grace found exclusively in His name.
