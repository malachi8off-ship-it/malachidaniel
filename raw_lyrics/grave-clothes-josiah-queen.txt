Grave Clothes
Josiah Queen
meaning
This song is a declaration of leaving the dead, sinful past behind and stepping into the resurrected life Christ offers. It uses the powerful biblical narrative of Jesus calling Lazarus from the tomb in John 11, telling him to take off his grave clothes.
