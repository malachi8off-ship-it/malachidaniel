You Hold It All Together
Maverick City Music
meaning
"You Hold It All Together" by Maverick City Music is a contemporary Christian worship song that emphasizes faith and trust in God's sovereign control during times of personal turmoil and uncertainty. Lyrically, the track reflects themes of divine provision, peace, and ultimate authority, drawing heavily on biblical passages such as Colossians 1:17, which declares that "in him all things hold together." Through its meditative melody and communal style, the song encourages believers to surrender anxiety by anchoring their confidence in the unchanging character and sustaining power of God.
