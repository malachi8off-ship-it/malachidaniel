Holy Forever
Chris Tomlin
meaning
"Holy Forever," a contemporary worship song by Chris Tomlin, is performed in English and invites believers to exalt God’s eternal holiness and unchanging nature, urging worshippers to respond with reverence and devotion. The lyrics draw upon biblical themes of divine transcendence—echoing passages such as Isaiah 6:3 and Revelation 4:8—to remind listeners that God’s holiness is the foundation of worship and the ultimate reality that shapes faith.
