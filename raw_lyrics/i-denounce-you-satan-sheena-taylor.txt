I Denounce You Satan
Sheena Taylor
meaning
"I Denounce You Satan," performed by Nigerian worship leader Sheena Taylor, is a contemporary Christian worship anthem that confronts the spiritual adversary with bold conviction, urging believers to reject Satan’s influence and embrace God's truth. The song’s core message draws on biblical themes of spiritual warfare—particularly passages such as 1 Peter 5:8 and Ephesians 6:12—emphasizing the believer’s authority in Christ to overcome demonic forces through faith and the power of the Holy Spirit. By blending passionate lyrics with a triumphant musical arrangement, the track serves as both a declaration of faith and a call to active resistance against evil.
