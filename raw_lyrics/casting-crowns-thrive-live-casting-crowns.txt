Casting Crowns - "Thrive" Live
Casting Crowns
meaning
This song encourages believers to go beyond mere survival and to flourish in their faith by being deeply rooted in God's Word. It is directly inspired by Psalm 1, depicting the righteous as a thriving tree planted by streams of water.
