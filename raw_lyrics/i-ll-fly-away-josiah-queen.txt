I'll Fly Away
Josiah Queen
meaning
This acoustic rendition of the classic hymn rejoices in the ultimate hope of escaping earthly sorrow for eternal life in heaven. It is rooted in the biblical promise of 1 Thessalonians 4, where believers will be caught up to be with the Lord forever.
