Good
Matthew West
meaning
Matthew West’s Christian worship song "Good" is an upbeat declaration of faith that encourages believers to trust in God's sovereignty and benevolent nature, even amidst life's trials and uncertainties. Drawing heavily from the biblical theme of Romans 8:28—which asserts that God works all things together for the good of those who love Him—the lyrics remind listeners that divine goodness is constant and independent of shifting human circumstances. Through an uplifting musical arrangement, the song reinforces the theological message that God's overarching purpose for humanity is fundamentally positive and faithful.
