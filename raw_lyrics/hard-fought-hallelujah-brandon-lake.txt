Hard Fought Hallelujah
Brandon Lake
meaning
This song acknowledges that true worship often comes from a place of deep struggle and enduring through the fire. It reflects the biblical concept of a 'sacrifice of praise' mentioned in Hebrews 13, worshiping even when it costs something.
