Phil Wickham - Heart Of Worship (Singalong 4 Live)
Phil Wickham
meaning
This song is about stripping away the performance and production of religion to return to a pure, Jesus-centered devotion. It reflects the biblical desire of God in Amos 5 and Psalm 51 for sincere hearts over outward religious displays.
