LEMONADE
Forrest Frank
meaning
"LEMONADE" by Christian artist Forrest Frank is an upbeat pop song centered on the theme of finding joy, gratitude, and divine perspective amidst life's trials. The track draws thematic inspiration from biblical principles such as Romans 8:28, which teaches that God works all things together for good, encouraging believers to metaphorically turn life's bitterness—represented by lemons—into something sweet through faith.
