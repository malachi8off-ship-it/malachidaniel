If We Are The Body
Casting Crowns
meaning
This song challenges the church to live out its calling to be the hands and feet of Jesus, welcoming the outcast and hurting. It is a direct critique based on 1 Corinthians 12, asking why the 'body of Christ' fails to act like Christ.
