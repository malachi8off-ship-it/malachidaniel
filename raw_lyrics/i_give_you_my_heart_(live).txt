I Give You My Heart (Live)
Hillsong Worship
meaning
I Give You My Heart (Live) by Hillsong Worship is a contemporary worship anthem that expresses a wholehearted surrender of love and devotion to God. The lyrics echo biblical themes of offering one’s heart to the Lord, drawing on passages such as Psalm 51:10 and 1 John 4:7‑8, which emphasize repentance, love, and the believer’s commitment to God. The live rendition captures the communal worship experience, inviting listeners to join in a heartfelt declaration of faith.
