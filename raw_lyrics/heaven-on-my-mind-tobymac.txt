meaning
"Heaven On My Mind" is a Christian worship song by TobyMac, an American Christian music artist. The song's core meaning revolves around the longing for heaven and the desire to be with God, drawing from biblical references such as Revelation 21:4, where it is written that God will wipe away every tear from their eyes, and there will be no more death or mourning or crying or pain. The song's lyrics convey a sense of hope and anticipation for eternal life with God, with no mention of it being sung in any language other than English.
