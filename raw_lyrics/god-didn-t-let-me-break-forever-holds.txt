God Didn't Let Me Break
Forever Holds
meaning
"God Didn't Let Me Break" by Forever Holds is a contemporary worship track that underscores God’s unwavering presence and strength amid personal trials, encouraging believers to trust in His sustaining grace. The lyrics draw on biblical themes of faithfulness, referencing passages such as Isaiah 41:10 and Psalm 46:1 to illustrate that even when life feels overwhelming, God’s love and power keep us from falling. The song invites listeners to find hope in God’s unchanging nature and to hold fast to His promise that He will never let them break.
