Gracefully Broken
Matt Redman; Tasha Cobbs Leonard
meaning
This powerful song describes the painful but beautiful process of surrendering one's pride to be molded by God. It embraces the biblical paradox found in Psalm 51, that the sacrifices God desires are a broken spirit and a contrite heart.
