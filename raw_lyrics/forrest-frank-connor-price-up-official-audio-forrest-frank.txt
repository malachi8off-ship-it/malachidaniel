"Forrest Frank & Connor Price’s worship track “UP!” (Official Audio) is an energetic, contemporary praise song that encourages believers to lift their hearts and voices to God, celebrating the power of the Holy Spirit to elevate and sustain them. The lyrics draw on biblical themes of exaltation and divine empowerment, echoing passages such as Psalm 103:1‑5 and Psalm 139:14, while inviting listeners to experience God’s presence and strength in their daily lives. The song is performed entirely in English, maintaining a clear, accessible style for congregational worship."
