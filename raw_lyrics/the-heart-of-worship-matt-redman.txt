The Heart Of Worship
Matt Redman
meaning
This iconic song strips away the music and production to return to the core essence of worship: a heart devoted to Jesus. It is a modern reflection of Psalm 51:16-17, acknowledging that God desires a broken and contrite heart over outward sacrifices.
