meaning
"Goodness of God" is a Christian worship song performed by CeCe Winans, drawing inspiration from the biblical account of Job's unwavering faith in the face of trials and tribulations (Job 1:20-22). The song's core meaning revolves around the theme of trusting in God's goodness and sovereignty, even in the midst of suffering, as expressed in the lyrics "You are good, You are good, When darkness falls, Your goodness remains." The song does not use any specific non-English language or style, and is a heartfelt expression of devotion and faith in God's goodness.
