Rescue Me
We the Kingdom
meaning
"Rescue Me" by We the Kingdom is a contemporary worship song that invites listeners to seek God's deliverance and trust in His faithfulness, emphasizing the believer’s dependence on divine rescue in times of distress. The lyrics draw on biblical themes of God's protection and salvation, echoing passages such as Psalm 34:17‑18, Psalm 91, and the New Testament portrayal of Jesus as the ultimate rescuer. The song’s message encourages a heartfelt reliance on God’s grace, underscoring the assurance that He is always ready to rescue those who call upon Him.
