Brandon Lake - Don't You Give Up On Me (Lyric Video)
Brandon Lake
meaning
This track is a vulnerable plea for God's patience and sustaining grace during moments of weakness. It reflects the biblical reality of God's enduring faithfulness, even when humans are faithless, as stated in 2 Timothy 2:13.
