Hillsong Worship
meaning
"Here I Am to Worship" by Hillsong Worship is a Christian worship song that conveys a sense of surrender and devotion to God. The song's lyrics, written by Tim Hughes, express a willingness to give oneself completely to God, drawing from biblical themes of worship and surrender, as seen in Isaiah 6:1-5, where the prophet Isaiah responds to God's presence with a declaration of his readiness to serve. This powerful anthem has been a staple in Christian worship services worldwide, inspiring believers to offer themselves as living sacrifices to God.
