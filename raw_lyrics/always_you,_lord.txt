Always You, Lord
Gravel & Grace
meaning
"Always You, Lord" by Gravel & Grace is a contemporary Christian worship song that centers on the theme of unwavering devotion and finding steadfast identity and comfort in God amidst life's shifting circumstances. Rooted in biblical passages that emphasize God's eternality and sovereignty—such as Psalm 90:2, which declares that "from everlasting to everlasting you are God"—the lyrics reinforce the theological concept that human purpose is found entirely in praising and relying on the Creator. Through its reflective and uplifting melody, the song serves as a communal declaration of faith, directing the worshiper's focus away from worldly distractions and toward the unchanging nature of the divine.
