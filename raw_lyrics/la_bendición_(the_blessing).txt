La Bendición (The Blessing)
Elevation Worship
meaning
"La Bendición (The Blessing)" by Elevation Worship is a heartfelt worship song that acts as a powerful prayer for divine favor, protection, and peace over individuals, their families, and future generations. Its core meaning centers on declaring and receiving God's unwavering blessing, drawing directly from the ancient Aaronic Priestly Blessing found in Numbers 6:24-26. The song encourages a confident affirmation of God's presence and grace, promising His goodness will follow and surround His people.
