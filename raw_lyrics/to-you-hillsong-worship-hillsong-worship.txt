To You - Hillsong Worship
Hillsong Worship
meaning
This worship anthem is a declaration of complete surrender, offering every aspect of life to God's service. It aligns with the biblical call to offer our bodies as a living sacrifice, holy and pleasing to God, found in Romans 12:1.
