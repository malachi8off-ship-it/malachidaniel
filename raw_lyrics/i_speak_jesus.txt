I Speak Jesus
Charity Gayle
meaning
"I Speak Jesus" by Charity Gayle is an English-language worship song that centers on the believer’s proclamation of Jesus’ name as a declaration of faith and trust, emphasizing the personal and communal power of speaking His name. The lyrics draw on biblical themes such as the identity of Christ as the Word (John 1:1) and the believer’s call to proclaim His name (Acts 1:8), framing the act of speaking Jesus as both a confession of identity and a proclamation of His redemptive work. The song’s core message encourages worshippers to affirm their relationship with Christ through vocal praise, reflecting the biblical call to “speak the truth in love” (Ephesians 4:15) and to live as witnesses of His grace.
