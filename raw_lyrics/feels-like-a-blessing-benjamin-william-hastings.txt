Feels Like A Blessing
Benjamin William Hastings
meaning
This song reflects on how difficult seasons, trials, and unmet expectations can ultimately be tools God uses for our good. It aligns with James 1, which encourages believers to consider it pure joy when facing trials because it produces endurance.
