Amazing Grace
John Newton
meaning
Written by former slave ship captain John Newton, "Amazing Grace" is a Christian hymn that celebrates the transformative power of divine forgiveness and redemption. Rooted in biblical themes of spiritual blindness, salvation by faith, and God's mercy as detailed in passages like Ephesians 2:8–9 and the story of the prodigal son, the song expresses profound gratitude for unearned grace. Its core meaning centers on the belief that no sinner is beyond the reach of God's saving love, offering comfort and hope through spiritual rebirth.
