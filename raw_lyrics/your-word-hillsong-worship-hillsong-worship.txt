Your Word - Hillsong Worship
Hillsong Worship
meaning
This anthem celebrates the absolute truth, guiding light, and eternal nature of the Holy Scriptures. It is a direct musical reflection of Psalm 119:105, declaring 'Your word is a lamp for my feet, a light on my path.'
