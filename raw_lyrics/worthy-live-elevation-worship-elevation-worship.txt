meaning
"Worthy" by Elevation Worship is a Christian worship song that expresses the worthiness of God and His love for humanity. The song's lyrics are centered around the theme of God's sovereignty and redemption, drawing from biblical passages such as Revelation 4:11, where God is declared worthy to receive all glory, honor, and power. The song's message emphasizes the believer's identity in Christ and their response to God's unwavering love and worthiness.
