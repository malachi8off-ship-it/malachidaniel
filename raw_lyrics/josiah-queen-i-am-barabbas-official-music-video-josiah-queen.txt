Josiah Queen
meaning
This song uses a powerful biblical metaphor to express the reality of grace, identifying the sinner with the guilty man who was set free when Jesus took his place. It is directly based on the Gospel accounts of the crucifixion, illustrating penal substitutionary atonement.
