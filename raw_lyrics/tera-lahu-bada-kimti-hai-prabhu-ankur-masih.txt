Tera Lahu Bada Kimti Hai Prabhu
Ankur Masih
meaning
Sung in Hindi, this deeply moving song praises the precious, cleansing, and redeeming power of Jesus' blood shed on the cross. It is firmly rooted in 1 Peter 1:18-19, which states we are redeemed by the precious blood of Christ, a lamb without blemish.
