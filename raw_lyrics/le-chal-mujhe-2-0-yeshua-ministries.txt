Le Chal Mujhe 2.0
Yeshua Ministries
meaning
Sung in Hindi, this worship song is a plea for God to lead the believer deeper into His presence and holy sanctuary. It reflects the journey of the Old Testament priests moving from the outer courts into the Holy of Holies to meet with God.
