The Cross (Official Lyric Video)
Anne Wilson
meaning
This song centers entirely on the finished work of Jesus on Calvary, celebrating the freedom and forgiveness found there. It reflects Paul's declaration in Galatians 6:14, choosing to boast in nothing except the cross of our Lord Jesus Christ.
