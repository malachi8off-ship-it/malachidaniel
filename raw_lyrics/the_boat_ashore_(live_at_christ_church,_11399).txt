The Boat Ashore (Live at Christ Church, 11/3/99)
The 77s
meaning
"The Boat Ashore (Live at Christ Church, 11/3/99)" by The 77s is a live Christian rock performance centered on the theme of spiritual rescue, surrender, and finding ultimate refuge in faith. Musically capturing an intimate church setting, the song uses the metaphorical imagery of a vessel safely reaching the shore to reflect the biblical journey of navigating life's trials and coming home to God. Its core message draws inspiration from New Testament accounts of Jesus calming the storms and guiding His disciples, symbolizing divine protection and peace found after spiritual turmoil.
