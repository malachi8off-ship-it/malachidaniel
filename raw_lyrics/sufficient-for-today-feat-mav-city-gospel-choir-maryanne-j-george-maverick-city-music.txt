Sufficient for Today (feat. Mav City Gospel Choir & Maryanne J. George)
Maverick City Music
meaning
This reassuring track reminds listeners that God provides exactly the amount of grace and strength needed for the present day. It leans heavily on Jesus' teaching in Matthew 6:34, trusting God for today's needs rather than worrying about tomorrow.
