The Human Race
Steven Curtis Chapman
meaning
This song reflects on the frantic pace of modern life and the necessity of finding God's grace amidst our striving. It echoes the biblical theme of running the spiritual race with endurance, looking to Jesus, as described in Hebrews 12.
