Phil Wickham - Battle Belongs (Official Lyric Video)
Phil Wickham
meaning
This powerful anthem encourages believers to fight their battles through worship and prayer, trusting God for the victory. It is rooted in the story of King Jehoshaphat in 2 Chronicles 20, where the Lord says, 'the battle is not yours, but God's.'
