CeCe Winans
meaning
CeCe Winans’ “Come Jesus Come” is an English-language worship anthem that invites listeners to call upon Jesus’ presence and experience His transformative power, echoing the biblical call to “come, Lord, let us worship” (Revelation 3:20) and the yearning expressed in Psalm 25:4. The song’s soaring melody and heartfelt lyrics underscore the Christian hope of Jesus’ return and the believer’s desire for intimate communion with Him, drawing on scriptural themes of repentance, redemption, and the promise of salvation.
