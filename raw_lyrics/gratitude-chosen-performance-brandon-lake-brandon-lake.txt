Gratitude (Chosen Performance) - Brandon Lake
Brandon Lake
meaning
"Gratitude (Chosen Performance)" by Brandon Lake is a contemporary worship song that celebrates God’s faithfulness and the believer’s response of thankfulness, emphasizing the biblical principle that gratitude is a natural outcome of experiencing God’s grace (1 Thessalonians 5:18, Psalm 107:1). The lyrics encourage listeners to lift their hearts in praise, recognizing that every blessing—both seen and unseen—originates from God’s loving providence, and they invite the congregation to join in a communal expression of thankfulness that reflects the heart of the New Testament church.
