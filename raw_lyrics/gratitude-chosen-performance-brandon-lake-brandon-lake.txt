Gratitude (Chosen Performance) - Brandon Lake
Brandon Lake
meaning
This intimate song expresses a feeling of inadequacy in bringing a worthy offering to God, settling on simple, authentic praise. It reflects the biblical truth that God desires sincere, heartfelt worship over empty religious sacrifices.
