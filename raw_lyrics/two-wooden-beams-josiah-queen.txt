Two Wooden Beams
Josiah Queen
meaning
This acoustic song focuses entirely on the physical cross of Christ, marveling at how a simple Roman execution device became the instrument of global salvation. It highlights the theology of Colossians 2, where God canceled the record of debt, nailing it to the cross.
