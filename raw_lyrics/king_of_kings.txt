King of Kings
Hillsong Worship
meaning
"King of Kings," a worship anthem by Hillsong Worship, celebrates the sovereignty and divinity of Jesus Christ, affirming His reign over all creation and His role as the ultimate ruler of the universe. The lyrics draw on biblical themes such as the New Testament portrayal of Christ as the Alpha and Omega (Revelation 1:8) and the prophetic declarations of His eternal kingship (Psalm 2:6‑7), inviting believers to worship Him as the victorious King who conquers sin and death. The song’s powerful refrain and soaring melodies aim to inspire worshippers to acknowledge Jesus’ supreme authority and to respond with adoration and devotion.
