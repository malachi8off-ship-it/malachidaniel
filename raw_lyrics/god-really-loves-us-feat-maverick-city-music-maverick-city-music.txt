God Really Loves Us (feat. Maverick City Music)
Maverick City Music
meaning
This collaborative worship anthem is a joyous celebration of the depth, reality, and sacrifice of God's love for humanity. It heavily relies on the truth of Romans 5:8, that God demonstrated His love by Christ dying for us while we were still sinners.
