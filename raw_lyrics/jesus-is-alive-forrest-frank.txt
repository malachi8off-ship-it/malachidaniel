JESUS IS ALIVE
Forrest Frank
meaning
"Jesus Is Alive" by Forrest Frank is a contemporary worship anthem that celebrates the resurrection of Jesus Christ, affirming His victory over death and the promise of eternal life for believers. The song lyrically emphasizes the living presence of Christ in the hearts of worshippers, encouraging a personal encounter with the risen Savior. Biblically, it draws on passages such as 1 Corinthians 15:3‑4 and Romans 6:4, underscoring the foundational Christian doctrine that Christ’s resurrection is the cornerstone of faith and hope.
