Let Everything That Has Breath
Matt Redman
meaning
This worship anthem is a universal call for all of creation to lift up their voices in praise to God. It is a direct lyrical adaptation of the final verse of the book of Psalms (Psalm 150:6): 'Let everything that has breath praise the Lord.'
