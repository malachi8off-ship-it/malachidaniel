Blind / Four Seven
Jars Of Clay
meaning
This acoustic-driven track highlights the struggle of human weakness and the necessity of divine illumination. It is conceptually anchored in 2 Corinthians 4:7, emphasizing that human frailty exists to show God's transcendent power.
