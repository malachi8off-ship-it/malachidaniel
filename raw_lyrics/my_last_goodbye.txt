My Last Goodbye
Sarah Hession
meaning
"My Last Goodbye" by Sarah Hession is a contemporary Christian worship song that explores the profound emotional weight of surrendering personal earthly attachments and grief to God. Rooted in the biblical themes of self-denial and eternal perspective found in passages like Matthew 16:25—which speaks of losing one's life for Christ's sake to truly find it—the song serves as a meditation on dying to oneself. Ultimately, it expresses a faithful resolution to release worldly control, trusting entirely in the hope of resurrection and eternal union with the divine.
