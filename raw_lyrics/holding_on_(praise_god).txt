Holding On (Praise God)
Jon Reddick
meaning
Jon Reddick’s contemporary worship song "Holding On (Praise God)" is an uplifting anthem centered on the spiritual discipline of choosing gratitude and praise during life's deepest trials. Drawing biblical inspiration from passages like Job 1:21 ("The Lord gives, and the Lord takes away; blessed be the name of the Lord") and the broader scriptural call to offer a "sacrifice of praise," the lyrics encourage believers to anchor their faith in God's unchanging character rather than shifting external circumstances. Ultimately, the song reassures listeners that holding onto God in difficult seasons—and lifting one's voice in worship despite pain—brings divine comfort and enduring spiritual strength.
