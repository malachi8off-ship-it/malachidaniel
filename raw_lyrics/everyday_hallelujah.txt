Everyday Hallelujah
Phil Wickham
meaning
"Everyday Hallelujah" by Phil Wickham is a contemporary Christian worship song that encourages believers to offer continuous, daily praise to God regardless of life's circumstances. Rooted in biblical themes of gratitude and steadfast devotion found in passages like Psalm 34:1 ("I will extol the Lord at all times"), the song emphasizes that worship is not merely a Sunday activity but a constant posture of the heart acknowledging God's holiness, faithfulness, and grace.
