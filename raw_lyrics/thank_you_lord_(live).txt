Thank You Lord (Live)
Hillsong Worship
meaning
"Thank You Lord (Live)" by Hillsong Worship is a contemporary Christian congregational song that expresses profound gratitude to God for His enduring mercy, grace, and redemption. Lyrically and conceptually, the song draws heavily from biblical themes of thanksgiving found throughout the Psalms—such as Psalm 107:1, which calls believers to give thanks to the Lord for His goodness and steadfast love. Through its live worship setting, the song encourages corporate praise, reflecting a biblical posture of humility and reverence in response to divine salvation and daily provision.
