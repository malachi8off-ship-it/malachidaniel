meaning
"Praise," a collaborative worship track by Elevation Worship featuring Brandon Lake, Chris Brown, and Chandler Moore, invites listeners to exalt God’s holiness and sovereignty through a soaring, contemporary musical arrangement. The lyrics emphasize the biblical call to worship—rooted in passages such as Psalm 95:1‑2 and Hebrews 12:28—to acknowledge God’s creative power and steadfast love, urging believers to lift their hearts in gratitude and reverence. The song’s uplifting melody and repetitive refrain reinforce the worshipful theme of praising God “for His love and grace” while encouraging a communal, heartfelt response to His divine presence.
