Raaja Hai Maahan
Sheldon Bangera
meaning
Sung in Hindi, this high-energy song is a celebration of the supreme greatness, power, and kingship of Jesus Christ. It reflects the declarations of the Psalms, continually exalting the Lord as the great King over all the earth.
