Promises
Maverick City Music
meaning
"Promises" by Maverick City Music is a contemporary Christian worship song that focuses on the faithfulness of God and the certainty of His biblical covenants throughout history. Drawing inspiration from various scriptures—such as the Genesis narrative of Noah's ark, the wilderness wanderings of the Israelites, and the resurrection of Jesus—the lyrics encourage believers to anchor their faith in God's unchanging nature and His historical track record of keeping His word. Through its uplifting melody and congregational style, the song serves as a reminder to trust divine providence during times of uncertainty and hardship.
