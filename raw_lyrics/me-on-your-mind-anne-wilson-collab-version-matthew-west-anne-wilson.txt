Me on Your Mind (Anne Wilson Collab Version)
Matthew West, Anne Wilson
meaning
This song marvels at the profound truth that Jesus went to the cross specifically thinking of and loving the individual believer. It reflects the deeply personal nature of Christ's sacrifice, echoing Paul's words in Galatians 2:20, 'who loved me and gave himself for me.'
