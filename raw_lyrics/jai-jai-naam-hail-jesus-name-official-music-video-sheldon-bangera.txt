Jai Jai Naam (Hail Jesus' Name) Official Music Video
Sheldon Bangera
meaning
Sung in Hindi, this triumphant song exalts the name of Jesus as the highest and most victorious name. It is grounded in Philippians 2:9-11, declaring that at the name of Jesus every knee should bow.
