What A Beautiful Name - Hillsong Worship
Hillsong Worship
meaning
This worship anthem glorifies the supremacy, wonder, and redemptive power of the name of Jesus Christ. It progresses from the wonder of creation to Christ's triumph over death, which reconciled humanity to God. The biblical foundation centers on the Christological exaltation in Philippians 2:9-11 and Colossians 1, describing the name above all names and the eternal word of God.
