God I’m Just Grateful (Radio Version)
Chandler Moore
meaning
"God I’m Just Grateful (Radio Version)" by Chandler Moore is a contemporary Christian worship song that expresses deep appreciation to God for His unearned grace, mercy, and sustained presence through life's trials. The song's core meaning centers on humble thanksgiving, shifting the believer's focus away from material requests and toward praise for spiritual redemption. This theme reflects the biblical call to gratitude found throughout Scripture, such as in 1 Thessalonians 5:18, which commands believers to give thanks in all circumstances, and Psalm 107:1, which urges giving thanks to the Lord for His enduring goodness.
