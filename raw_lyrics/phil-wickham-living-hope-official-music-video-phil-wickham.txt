Phil Wickham - Living Hope (Official Music Video)
Phil Wickham
meaning
Phil Wickham’s “Living Hope” is an upbeat worship anthem that celebrates the assurance of Christ’s resurrection and the eternal hope it brings, echoing the promise in Romans 8:24‑25 that “the hope that we have is not a mere wish but a confident expectation.” The lyrics emphasize that this hope is “alive” and “unshakable,” encouraging believers to hold fast to the promise of new life in Christ, while the music video visually reinforces the themes of renewal and the triumph of faith over despair.
