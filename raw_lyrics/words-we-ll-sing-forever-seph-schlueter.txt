Words We'll Sing Forever
Seph Schlueter
meaning
This track anticipates the eternal worship in heaven, declaring that the praises we sing to God now will continue forever. It reflects the eternal chorus depicted in Revelation, where the saints endlessly sing 'Worthy is the Lamb.'
