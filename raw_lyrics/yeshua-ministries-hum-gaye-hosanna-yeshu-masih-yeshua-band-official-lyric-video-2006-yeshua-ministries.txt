Yeshua Ministries - Hum Gaye Hosanna (Yeshu Masih) (Yeshua Band) Official Lyric Video 2006
Yeshua Ministries
meaning
Sung in Hindi, this classic anthem is a joyful shout of 'Hosanna,' welcoming the presence and kingship of Jesus. It directly mirrors the Triumphal Entry in Matthew 21, where the crowds praised Christ as the coming King.
