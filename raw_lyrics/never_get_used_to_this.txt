NEVER GET USED TO THIS
Forrest Frank
meaning
This song expresses awe and profound gratitude for salvation, celebrating the believer's deliverance from spiritual death and darkness into life. The lyrics highlight God's perfect timing and faithfulness in breaking chains, framing the believer's response as unceasing, eternal praise alongside angels.
