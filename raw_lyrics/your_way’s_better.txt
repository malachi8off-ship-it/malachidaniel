YOUR WAY’S BETTER
Forrest Frank
meaning
"YOUR WAY'S BETTER" by Christian artist Forrest Frank is an upbeat contemporary worship song that explores themes of trust, surrender, and divine guidance. The track encourages believers to relinquish personal control and anxiety, affirming confidence in God's sovereign plan over their own desires. Its core message is deeply rooted in biblical principles such as Proverbs 3:5–6—which urges individuals to trust in the Lord with all their hearts rather than relying on their own understanding—and Romans 8:28, which assures believers that God works all things together for the good of those who love Him.
