Touch Of Faith
Joy Williams
meaning
This song is about reaching out to Jesus in desperate faith, trusting that even the smallest contact with Him brings healing. It is directly inspired by the biblical account of the woman with the issue of blood in Mark 5, who was healed by touching the hem of His garment.
