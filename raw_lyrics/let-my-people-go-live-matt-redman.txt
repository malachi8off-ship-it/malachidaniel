Let My People Go (Live)
Matt Redman
meaning
This song is a prophetic cry for freedom from spiritual captivity and injustice, invoking God's delivering power. It draws heavily on the Exodus narrative, echoing God's command to Pharaoh through Moses to release His people from bondage.
