Tell me, how is Heaven
May the Lord Studio
meaning
The worship song “Tell me, how is Heaven” by May the Lord Studio invites listeners to contemplate the nature of the eternal kingdom, expressing a yearning to experience God’s glory beyond earthly life. Rooted in biblical imagery from Revelation 21 and 2 Corinthians 5:1, the lyrics emphasize the promise of a new heavens and earth where believers will dwell in the presence of God forever. Through its simple, melodic refrain, the song encourages believers to trust in the hope of salvation and to seek the indescribable beauty of the divine realm.
