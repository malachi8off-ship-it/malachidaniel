Phil Wickham - Hymn Of Heaven (Official Lyric Video)
Phil Wickham
meaning
This majestic song anticipates the glorious day when all pain will cease, and believers will worship face-to-face with Christ. It is a vivid musical depiction of the eschatological promises found in Revelation 21, where God wipes away every tear.
