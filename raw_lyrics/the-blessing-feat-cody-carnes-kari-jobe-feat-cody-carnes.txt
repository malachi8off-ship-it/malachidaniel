The Blessing (feat. Cody Carnes)
Kari Jobe feat. Cody Carnes
meaning
"The Blessing" is a Christian worship song by Kari Jobe and Cody Carnes, which draws inspiration from the biblical concept of God's blessing and protection as described in Psalm 127:3. The song's core meaning revolves around the idea that God's presence and blessing are a shield that protects and provides for His children, and that He is a good Father who desires to bless and prosper them. The song's message is rooted in the biblical promise that God's blessing is a source of strength, comfort, and peace.
