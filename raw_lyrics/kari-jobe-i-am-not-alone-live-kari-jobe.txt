Kari Jobe - I Am Not Alone (Live)
Kari Jobe
meaning
This song provides immense comfort by declaring that God goes before us and stays with us through the darkest valleys. It is a direct lyrical application of Psalm 23, 'even though I walk through the darkest valley, I will fear no evil, for you are with me.'
