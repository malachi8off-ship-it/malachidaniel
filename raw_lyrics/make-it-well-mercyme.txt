meaning
'Make It Well' is a Christian worship song by the American band MercyMe, which conveys a message of trust and surrender to God's sovereignty in the midst of suffering and adversity. The song's core meaning is rooted in the biblical concept of God's goodness and redemption, as expressed in passages such as Psalm 23 and Romans 8:28, which remind believers that God works all things together for their good. The song's lyrics reflect a heart of faith and dependence on God's loving care, even in the darkest of times.
