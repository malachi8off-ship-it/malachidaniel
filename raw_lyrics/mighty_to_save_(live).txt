Mighty to Save (Live)
Hillsong Worship
meaning
"Mighty to Save (Live)" by Hillsong Worship is a contemporary worship anthem that celebrates the power and grace of Jesus Christ to rescue humanity from sin and despair. The lyrics draw directly from biblical themes of redemption—particularly the New Testament passages that emphasize Christ’s sacrificial love and the believer’s new life in Him—while encouraging congregants to trust in God’s sovereign salvation. The live recording captures the communal worship experience, underscoring the song’s message that God’s mercy is both mighty and accessible to all who call upon Him.
