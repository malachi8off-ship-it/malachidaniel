Goodness of God (Live)
CeCe Winans
meaning
"Goodness of God (Live)" by CeCe Winans is a contemporary Christian worship song that celebrates God's unwavering faithfulness, daily mercy, and constant protection throughout a believer's life. Rooted in biblical themes of divine grace and steadfast love—echoing passages such as Psalm 23 and Psalm 27:13, which speak of God's goodness following His people all the days of their lives—the song serves as a personal testimony and a communal declaration of total surrender and gratitude. Through its soaring live arrangement, the track invites congregations to reflect on a lifetime of divine provision and respond with a life-long offering of praise.
