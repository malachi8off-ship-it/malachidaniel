Killed A Man
Zach Williams
meaning
This gritty track uses the metaphor of 'killing a man' to describe the radical spiritual death of the old, sinful self upon coming to Christ. It is a powerful musical interpretation of Galatians 2:20, 'I have been crucified with Christ and I no longer live.'
