O Holy Night
Christmas Songs
meaning
"O Holy Night" is a classic Christmas carol that celebrates the birth of Jesus Christ and the redemption of humanity as prophesied in the Gospels. Its core meaning centers on the awe, spiritual awakening, and radical equality brought by the incarnation of the Savior, which breaks the chains of sin and oppression. Drawing heavily on biblical themes of the Nativity—such as the guiding Star of Bethlehem, the humble manger, and the proclamation of "peace on earth, goodwill to men"—the song invites believers to rejoice in the divine light that entered a darkened world.
