Revelation
Third Day
meaning
This song is an earnest prayer for God to provide clarity, direction, and spiritual insight during confusing times. It reflects the Apostle Paul's prayer in Ephesians 1, asking God to give a Spirit of wisdom and revelation to know Him better.
