Make me Whole Again (Nothing but the Blood of Jesus)
Sammy Ward
meaning
Sammy Ward's contemporary Christian worship song "Make Me Whole Again (Nothing but the Blood of Jesus)" centers on the theological theme of redemption and spiritual cleansing through the sacrificial death of Jesus Christ. Musically and lyrically, the song draws heavily on traditional evangelical motifs and biblical passages such as Hebrews 9:22 and 1 John 1:7, which emphasize that forgiveness of sin is attained exclusively through Christ's blood. Ultimately, the track serves as a devotional expression of human brokenness and the belief in divine restoration.
