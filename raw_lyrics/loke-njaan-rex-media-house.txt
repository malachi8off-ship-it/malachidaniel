Loke Njaan
Rex Media House
meaning
Loke Njaan, a worship anthem by Rex Media House, is sung in Malayalam and invites believers to recognize Jesus as the Lord of all creation, echoing the biblical affirmation that He reigns over the heavens and earth (Psalm 47:1). The song emphasizes the call to worship Him with all our hearts, aligning with the New Testament exhortation to praise God in spirit and truth (John 4:24).
