Right In The Middle (with Hulvey)
Brandon Lake
meaning
This collaborative track assures listeners that God does not wait for us to have it all together, but meets us right in the middle of our mess. It reflects the biblical truth of Romans 5:8, that while we were still sinners, Christ died for us.
