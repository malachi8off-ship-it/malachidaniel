Above All
Michael W. Smith
meaning
This powerful worship anthem exalts Jesus above all earthly powers, kingdoms, and created things, before reflecting on His crucifixion. It beautifully captures the Christological hymn of Philippians 2, highlighting Christ's supreme majesty and humble sacrifice.
