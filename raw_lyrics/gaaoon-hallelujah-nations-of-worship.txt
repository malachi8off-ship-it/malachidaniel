Gaaoon Hallelujah
Nations of Worship
meaning
Sung in Hinglish, this worship song is a declaration of singing 'Hallelujah' as an ultimate expression of praise and victory in Christ. It echoes the heavenly chorus found in Revelation 19, praising God for His salvation.
