Tere Bina
Yeshua Ministries
meaning
Sung in Hindi, this track is a heartfelt declaration that life is utterly meaningless and impossible without the presence of Jesus. It directly applies Christ's teaching in John 15, 'I am the vine; you are the branches... apart from me you can do nothing.'
