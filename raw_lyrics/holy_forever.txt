Holy Forever
Chris Tomlin
meaning
"Holy Forever" by Chris Tomlin is a contemporary Christian worship song that centers on the eternal holiness of God and the unending praise He receives from all creation. The lyrics draw heavily from biblical imagery found in the Book of Revelation, specifically echoing the unceasing worship of the living creatures and elders around God's throne, as well as Old Testament themes of God's majesty and faithfulness. Ultimately, the song serves as a congregational anthem that unites believers in the timeless act of exalting God alongside the heavenly host.
