This Baby
Steven Curtis Chapman
meaning
This Christmas song marvels at the paradox of the almighty Creator of the universe becoming a vulnerable infant. It reflects the profound Christological theology of Philippians 2, where Christ made Himself nothing by taking the very nature of a servant.
