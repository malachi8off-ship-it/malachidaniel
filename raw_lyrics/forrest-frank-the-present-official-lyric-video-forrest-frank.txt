Forrest Frank - THE PRESENT (Official Lyric Video)
Forrest Frank
meaning
This track emphasizes the importance of living in the current moment God has given us, free from the worries of tomorrow. It is firmly based on Jesus' teaching in Matthew 6:34 to not worry about tomorrow, for today has enough trouble of its own.
