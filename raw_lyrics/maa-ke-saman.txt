Maa Ke Saman
Bridge Music
meaning
"Maa Ke Saman" by Bridge Music is a contemporary Christian worship song that praises the unfailing, nurturing, and protective love of God, likening it to the unconditional comfort of a mother. Drawing upon biblical imagery of divine care—such as Isaiah 66:13, which compares God's comfort to that of a mother—the song expresses themes of deep devotion, surrender, and finding ultimate security in God's presence. Through its gentle melodies and reflective lyrics, the track invites believers to experience the profound peace and intimacy of being held by their Creator.
