Can't Steal My Joy (feat. Brandon Lake)
Josiah Queen/Brandon Lake
meaning
This song is a defiant declaration that the enemy and worldly trials cannot take away the joy given by God. It is rooted in Nehemiah 8:10, declaring that the joy of the Lord is the believer's strength.
