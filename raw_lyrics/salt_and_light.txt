Salt & Light
Lauren Daigle
meaning
"Salt & Light" by Lauren Daigle is a contemporary Christian worship song that encourages believers to actively live out their faith by positively influencing the world around them. Based on the Sermon on the Mount in Matthew 5:13–16, the lyrics call Christians to be the "salt of the earth" and the "light of the world," urging them to let their actions reflect God’s love and truth. The song serves as an inspirational reminder of the believer's responsibility to embody Christlike character and bring spiritual vitality to a secular culture.
