Michael W. Smith - Healing Rain
Michael W. Smith
meaning
This song is a metaphorical plea for God's Spirit to wash over the brokenness of the world and bring restoration. It reflects the biblical concept of the Holy Spirit's refreshing and healing presence, akin to rain on dry ground.
