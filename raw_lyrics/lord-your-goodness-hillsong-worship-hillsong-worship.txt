Lord Your Goodness - Hillsong Worship
Hillsong Worship
meaning
This song is a joyful reflection on the unending grace, mercy, and goodness that God pours out on His children. It echoes the psalmist's declaration in Psalm 23 that surely goodness and mercy will follow the believer all the days of their life.
