Anand Ki Bharpuri
Sheldon Bangera
meaning
Sung in Hindi, this joyful song celebrates the overwhelming joy and fulfillment found only in God's presence. It is directly inspired by Psalm 16:11, which states that in God's presence there is fullness of joy.
