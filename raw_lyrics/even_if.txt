Even If
MercyMe
meaning
Released in 2017 by the contemporary Christian band MercyMe, the worship song "Even If" explores the struggle of maintaining faith and trust in God during times of intense suffering and unanswered prayers. Drawing direct biblical inspiration from the account of Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego in Daniel 3—where the trio declares their faith in God's power to save them, yet resolves to remain faithful even if He chooses not to—the song affirms that genuine worship is not contingent upon receiving earthly healing or deliverance. Ultimately, the lyrics emphasize an unwavering reliance on God's sovereignty and goodness, regardless of life's painful outcomes.
