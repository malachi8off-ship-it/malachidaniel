Rex Media House
meaning
"Paadidaam Hallelujah," a Christian worship song produced by Rex Media House, is a joyful call for believers to lift their voices in praise and exaltation of God. Rooted in the traditional biblical themes of the Book of Psalms—frequently echoing exhortations like Psalm 146 ("Praise the Lord, my soul; I will praise the Lord all my life")—the song emphasizes communal worship and gratitude for God’s enduring goodness and sovereignty. Through its uplifting melodies and vernacular lyrics, the piece serves to inspire spiritual renewal and congregational devotion within the South Indian Christian tradition.
