Rise Above
Kevin Quinn
meaning
"Rise Above" by Kevin Quinn is a contemporary Christian worship song that encourages believers to overcome personal struggles, doubt, and earthly distractions by fixing their focus on God. Rooted in biblical themes of spiritual renewal and perseverance—drawing conceptually from passages like Colossians 3:2, which urges believers to set their minds on things above—the song serves as a reflective anthem of faith and reliance on divine strength. Through its uplifting message, the track calls listeners to rise above trials and live out a renewed purpose anchored in Christ.
