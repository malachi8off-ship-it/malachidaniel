Rest on Us (feat. Brandon Lake & Eniola Abioye)
Maverick City Music
meaning
This worship song is a passionate invocation for the Holy Spirit to descend and rest upon the congregation. It recalls the biblical event of the Spirit descending like a dove on Jesus, and the tongues of fire at Pentecost in Acts 2.
