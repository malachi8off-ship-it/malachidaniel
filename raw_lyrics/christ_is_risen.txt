Christ Is Risen
Phil Wickham
meaning
Phil Wickham’s contemporary Christian worship song "Christ Is Risen" proclaims the resurrection of Jesus Christ and its triumphant victory over sin and death. Drawing heavily upon New Testament resurrection narratives, particularly the Gospel accounts of the empty tomb and Paul's writings in 1 Corinthians 15, the lyrics celebrate the foundational Christian belief that Christ's defeat of the grave offers eternal life and redemption to humanity. Through its upbeat tempo and congregational praise format, the song encourages believers to respond to this pivotal biblical event with communal joy, worship, and unwavering faith.
