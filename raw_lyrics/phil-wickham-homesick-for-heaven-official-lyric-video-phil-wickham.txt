Phil Wickham
meaning
Phil Wickham’s “Homesick For Heaven” is a contemporary worship anthem that expresses a deep yearning for eternal communion with God, echoing the Christian hope of being “in the presence of the Lord” (Revelation 21:3). The lyrics weave together scriptural imagery of the promised new heavens and new earth, encouraging believers to keep their focus on the eternal reward while living faithfully in the present. The song’s message is rooted in biblical themes of longing, redemption, and the assurance of God’s ultimate restoration, making it a popular choice for worship services that emphasize the hope of heaven.
