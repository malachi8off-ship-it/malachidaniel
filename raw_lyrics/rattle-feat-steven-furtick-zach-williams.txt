RATTLE! (feat. Steven Furtick)
Zach Williams
meaning
This explosive song celebrates God's power to bring dead things back to life, whether it's Jesus rising from the grave or our own spiritual revival. It is intensely inspired by Ezekiel 37, where the dry bones hear the word of the Lord and come together with a rattling sound.
