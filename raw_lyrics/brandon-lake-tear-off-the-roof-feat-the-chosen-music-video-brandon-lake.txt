Brandon Lake - TEAR OFF THE ROOF (feat. The Chosen) (Music Video)
Brandon Lake
meaning
This song celebrates radical, desperate faith that overcomes any obstacle to get to Jesus. It is directly based on the biblical account in Mark 2 where friends tear through a roof to lower a paralyzed man to Christ.
