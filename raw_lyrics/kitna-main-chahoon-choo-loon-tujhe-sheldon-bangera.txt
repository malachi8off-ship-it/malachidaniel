Kitna Main Chahoon Choo Loon Tujhe
Sheldon Bangera
meaning
Sheldon Bangera's worship song 'Kitna Main Chahoon Choo Loon Tujhe' is a Hindi/Hinglish expression of profound longing for intimate communion with God. It conveys a worshiper's deep desire to draw near and experience the tangible presence of the divine, echoing biblical sentiments of seeking God's face and thirsting for His touch, as seen in Psalms and the New Testament calls to approach God's throne of grace.
