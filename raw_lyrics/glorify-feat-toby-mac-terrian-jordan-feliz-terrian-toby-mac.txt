Glorify (feat. Toby Mac & Terrian)
Jordan Feliz, TERRIAN, Toby Mac
meaning
This upbeat track is a joyful commitment to make God's name famous and give Him the honor in every aspect of life. It reflects the biblical mandate in 1 Corinthians 10:31 to do all things for the glory of God.
