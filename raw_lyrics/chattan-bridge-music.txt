meaning
"Chattan" by Bridge Music is a **Hindi** worship song that exalts God as an unshakeable "Rock" and steadfast refuge. Drawing on rich biblical imagery prevalent in Psalms and the New Testament, it expresses profound trust in His unchanging nature as the ultimate foundation and source of strength for believers amidst life's challenges and uncertainties.
