My Redeemer Lives
Hillsong Worship
meaning
"My Redeemer Lives" is a contemporary worship anthem by Hillsong Worship that celebrates the victory and eternal life of Jesus Christ, emphasizing His resurrection as the cornerstone of Christian hope. The lyrics draw from biblical passages such as 1 Corinthians 15:20‑22 and Revelation 1:18, underscoring that Christ’s death and resurrection defeat sin and death, ensuring believers’ future resurrection. The song invites worshippers to proclaim the living Savior’s power and to live in the assurance of His ongoing presence.
