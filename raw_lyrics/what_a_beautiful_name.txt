What a Beautiful Name
Hillsong Worship
meaning
"What a Beautiful Name" by Hillsong Worship is a profound worship anthem that extols the unparalleled power, glory, and saving grace of Jesus Christ, detailing His divine nature, His role as Creator, His sacrificial death, and His victorious resurrection. Rooted deeply in scriptures such as Philippians 2:9-11, Colossians 1:15-20, and John 1:1-5, the song culminates in the declaration that Jesus' name is supreme above all others and holds ultimate redemptive authority for all eternity.
