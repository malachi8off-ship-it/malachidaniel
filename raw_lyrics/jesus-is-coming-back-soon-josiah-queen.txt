JESUS IS COMING BACK SOON
Josiah Queen
meaning
This song is an urgent, expectant reminder of the eschatological hope of Christ's return to make all things new. It draws upon the promises in Revelation 22, where Jesus declares, 'Yes, I am coming soon.'
