Sang Tere
Bridge Music
meaning
"Sang Tere" by Bridge Music is a Christian worship song primarily sung in Hindi with elements of English (Hinglish), expressing a profound desire for God's constant presence and companionship. It articulates a longing to walk with the Lord and find peace, strength, and refuge in Him, reflecting biblical themes of God as a faithful shepherd and ever-present help (e.g., Psalm 23, Matthew 28:20).
