What An Awesome God
Phil Wickham
meaning
Released on Phil Wickham's 2019 album *Living Hope*, "What An Awesome God" is a contemporary Christian worship song that celebrates the supreme majesty, holiness, and unmatched power of God. Drawing thematic inspiration from biblical passages such as Nehemiah 1:5 and Psalm 47:2—which describe God as great, awesome, and the King over all the earth—the lyrics invite believers to join in reverent awe and corporate praise. Ultimately, the song emphasizes humanity's response of worship in recognition of God's sovereign authority and gracious character.
