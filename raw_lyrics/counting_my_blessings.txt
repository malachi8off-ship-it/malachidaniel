Counting My Blessings
Seph Schlueter
meaning
Released in 2023 by contemporary Christian artist Seph Schlueter, "Counting My Blessings" is an upbeat worship song that encourages believers to focus on God's daily goodness and provision rather than their hardships. The track's core message reflects the biblical principle of gratitude found in passages like 1 Thessalonians 5:18, which commands Christians to give thanks in all circumstances. Through its catchy melody and reflective lyrics, the song serves as a reminder to actively recognize and praise God for both tangible and spiritual blessings.
