Phil Wickham - Your Love Awakens Me (Lyric Video)
Phil Wickham
meaning
This upbeat anthem describes the resurrecting power of God's love that calls believers from spiritual death to vibrant life. It echoes the Apostle Paul's words in Ephesians 2, that God, being rich in mercy, made us alive with Christ.
