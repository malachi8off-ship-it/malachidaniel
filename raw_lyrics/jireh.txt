Jireh
Elevation Worship
meaning
"Jireh" by Elevation Worship (featuring Maverick City Music) is a Christian worship song centered on the belief that God is the ultimate provider, sufficient for all needs. Its core message, reflected in the biblical name "Jehovah-Jireh" (meaning "The Lord Will Provide" from Genesis 22), assures listeners of God's boundless care and faithfulness. The song consequently fosters trust, contentment, and freedom from worry, encouraging reliance on divine provision over human striving, echoing themes found in passages like Philippians 4:19.
