Through It All
Hillsong Worship
meaning
The Christian worship song 'Through It All' is a popular anthem by Hillsong Worship, drawing inspiration from the biblical account of Job's unwavering faith in the face of trials and tribulations (Job 1:21). The song's core meaning revolves around trusting God's sovereignty and goodness, even in the midst of adversity, as expressed in the lyrics "Through it all, through every up and down, I'll trust in You." The song's message is a testament to the enduring power of faith and the unwavering hope found in God's presence.
