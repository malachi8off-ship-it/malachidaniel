Praise You In This Storm
Casting Crowns
meaning
This deeply emotional song is a choice to lift hands in worship even amidst profound grief, loss, and unanswered questions. It is heavily inspired by Job's response to tragedy, choosing to bless the Lord when everything is taken away.
