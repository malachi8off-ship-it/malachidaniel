Fear Is a Liar (Official Music Video)
Zach Williams
meaning
This powerful song exposes the lies that fear, anxiety, and the enemy use to keep believers feeling unworthy and trapped. It points back to John 8:44, identifying the devil as the father of lies, and countering it with God's truth.
