Huzoori
Ankur Masih
meaning
Sung in Hindi, this intimate song expresses a deep yearning to dwell continually in the holy presence of God. It echoes the heart of David in Psalm 27, seeking one thing: to dwell in the house of the Lord all the days of his life.
