Friend Of God
Israel & New Breed
meaning
"Friend of God" by Israel & New Breed is a contemporary Christian worship song that celebrates the profound intimacy and access believers have with God through faith. The song's core meaning centers on the transformative theological concept that the Almighty Creator condescends to call humanity His friends, drawing heavily from the biblical narrative in John 15:15, where Jesus tells His disciples He no longer calls them servants but friends, as well as the Old Testament description of Abraham being called the friend of God.
