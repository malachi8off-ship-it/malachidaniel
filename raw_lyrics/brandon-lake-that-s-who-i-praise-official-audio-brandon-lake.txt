meaning
"Brandon Lake – “That’s Who I Praise” is an English-language worship track that celebrates God’s steadfast love, mercy, and faithfulness, inviting listeners to lift their voices in gratitude for His grace and redemption. The song’s lyrical themes echo biblical passages such as Psalm 103’s declaration of God’s compassion, Psalm 145’s affirmation of His goodness, and the New Testament call to praise Jesus for the salvation He offers. By weaving these scriptural references into contemporary worship, Lake encourages believers to acknowledge God’s enduring presence and the transformative power of His love in their lives.
