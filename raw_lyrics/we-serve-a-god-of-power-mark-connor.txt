We Serve a God of Power
Mark Connor
meaning
This worship song is a confident declaration of God's omnipotence and His ability to perform miracles today. It reflects the biblical truth found in Jeremiah 32:17, that God made the heavens and the earth by His great power, and nothing is too hard for Him.
