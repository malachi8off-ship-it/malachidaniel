Seph Schlueter - Counting My Blessings (Lyric Video)
Seph Schlueter
meaning
Seph Schlueter’s “Counting My Blessings” is a contemporary worship lyric video that encourages believers to actively recognize and thank God for His daily provisions, echoing the biblical call to gratitude found in passages such as 1 Thessalonians 5:18 and Psalm 103. The song’s refrain of “counting my blessings” underscores the practice of mindful remembrance of God’s faithfulness, inviting listeners to shift their focus from scarcity to abundance. By pairing upbeat instrumentation with scriptural themes of thankfulness, the track serves as a modern reminder that worship can be expressed through both praise and the conscious acknowledgment of God’s blessings.
