Hosanna - Hillsong Worship
Hillsong Worship
meaning
This powerful anthem is a plea for revival, asking God to break our hearts for what breaks His and to cleanse our hands. It draws upon the crowds' cry of 'Hosanna' (Save us now) in Matthew 21 and the call for clean hands in Psalm 24.
