The Power of Your Love
Hillsong Worship
meaning
"The Power of Your Love" by Hillsong Worship is a contemporary worship song that celebrates the transformative and sustaining nature of God's love, inviting listeners to experience its redemptive power in their lives. The lyrics draw on biblical themes of divine love and grace, echoing passages such as John 3:16, 1 John 4:8, and Romans 5:5 to underscore how God's love is both the source of salvation and the force that empowers believers to live faithfully. The song’s message encourages worshippers to trust in God’s unending love as a source of strength and hope.
