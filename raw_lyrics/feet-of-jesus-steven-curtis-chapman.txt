Feet of Jesus
Steven Curtis Chapman
meaning
This track describes the posture of finding peace, forgiveness, and purpose by humbly submitting to Christ. It draws heavily on the biblical story of Mary of Bethany, who chose the 'better part' by sitting at the feet of Jesus in Luke 10.
