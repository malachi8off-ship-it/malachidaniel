Yeshu Naam And What A Beautiful Name
Yeshua Ministries
meaning
Sung in Hinglish, this mashup worships the matchless, beautiful, and powerful name of Jesus Christ. It is rooted in Philippians 2, declaring that God exalted Him to the highest place and gave Him the name that is above every name.
