Rescue
Lauren Daigle
meaning
"Rescue" is a contemporary Christian worship song by Lauren Daigle that explores themes of divine comfort, redemption, and unwavering support during times of deep personal struggle and isolation. The song serves as a musical paraphrase of biblical themes found in passages such as Isaiah 43—which promises that God will call His people by name and protect them through turbulent waters—and Psalm 91, emphasizing deliverance from fear and despair. Ultimately, the lyrics convey the theological message that no individual is beyond the reach of God's grace, offering reassurance that divine intervention is present even in the darkest circumstances.
