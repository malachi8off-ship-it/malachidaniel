God Rest Ye Merry Gentlemen
Jars of Clay
meaning
This alternative rendition of the classic carol celebrates the birth of Jesus and the comfort He brings by saving humanity from Satan's power. It highlights the biblical narrative of the nativity in Luke 2, proclaiming tidings of comfort and joy.
