The Jesus I Know
Benjamin William Hastings
meaning
This track contrasts the judgmental, religious caricatures of Jesus with the compassionate, grace-filled Savior found in the Gospels. It highlights the biblical Jesus who ate with sinners and offered rest to the weary, rejecting pharisaical legalism.
