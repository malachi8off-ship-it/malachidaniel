Way Maker
Sinach
meaning
"Way Maker" is a contemporary Christian worship song written by Nigerian gospel singer Sinach, which emphasizes the unwavering presence, faithfulness, and miraculous nature of God during difficult times. Drawing heavily on biblical themes of divine guidance and comfort—frequently echoing imagery of God making a way through the wilderness and parting seas as seen in the Exodus narrative—the lyrics serve as a congregational declaration of faith in God's ability to work behind the scenes even when he cannot be seen. Released in 2015, the song achieved global acclaim and has since become a staple in modern worship services worldwide, celebrated for its simple, repetitive message of hope and reassurance.
