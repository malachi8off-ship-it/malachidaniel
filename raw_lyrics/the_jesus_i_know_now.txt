The Jesus I Know Now
Brandon Lake
meaning
"The Jesus I Know Now" by Brandon Lake is a contemporary worship song that explores the transformative power of personal encounters with Christ, contrasting a former, distant understanding of God with a present, intimate relationship. The song's themes draw from biblical concepts of spiritual awakening and personal testimony, notably reflecting the Apostle Paul's journey in Philippians 3:8-10, where he emphasizes the surpassing worth of knowing Christ Jesus personally over rigid religious observance. Through an uplifting and reflective melody, the lyrics invite listeners to reflect on their own spiritual growth and the life-changing grace found in a deepened faith.
