WAARIS
Bridge Music
meaning
WAARIS, a worship song by Bridge Music, is performed in Hindi and centers on the believer’s identity as a rightful heir to God’s promises, urging listeners to claim their inheritance in Christ. Rooted in biblical themes such as Ephesians 1:13‑14 and Romans 8:17, the lyrics encourage faith, hope, and the assurance of God’s eternal kingdom.
