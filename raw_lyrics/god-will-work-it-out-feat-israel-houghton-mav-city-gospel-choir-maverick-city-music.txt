God Will Work It Out (feat. Israel Houghton & Mav City Gospel Choir)
Maverick City Music
meaning
This gospel-infused track is a declaration of unwavering faith that God will sovereignly resolve difficult circumstances for our good. It is completely anchored in Romans 8:28, trusting that all things work together for good for those who love God.
