Valentine - Hillsong Worship
Hillsong Worship
meaning
This song is an intimate expression of deep love and devotion to God, viewing Him as the ultimate lover of the soul. It draws upon the romantic, covenantal imagery found in the Song of Solomon, representing the relationship between Christ and the Church.
