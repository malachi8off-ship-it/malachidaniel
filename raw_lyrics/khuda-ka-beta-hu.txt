Khuda ka Beta Hu
Nations of Worship
meaning
"Khuda ka Beta Hu" (I Am the Son of God) by Nations of Worship is a contemporary Christian worship song that celebrates the believer's identity and adoption into the family of God through Jesus Christ. Drawing heavily from biblical themes of grace, redemption, and spiritual inheritance—such as those found in Romans 8:15–16 and John 1:12—the lyrics express themes of unconditional love, acceptance, and the intimate relationship between the believer and the Creator. Through its uplifting melody and congregational style, the song serves as a declaration of faith that encourages worshipers to embrace their spiritual identity and worth as children of God.
