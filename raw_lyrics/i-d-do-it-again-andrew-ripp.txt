I'd Do It Again
Andrew Ripp
meaning
Andrew Ripp’s worship song “I’d Do It Again” expresses a heartfelt desire to repeatedly serve God, echoing the biblical call to wholehearted devotion found in Psalm 95:7 and the New Testament exhortation to love and obey. The lyrics emphasize gratitude for God’s grace and a willingness to surrender again, reflecting the Christian commitment to continual worship and obedience.
