Homesick For Heaven
Phil Wickham
meaning
"Homesick for Heaven" by Phil Wickham is a contemporary Christian worship song that expresses a believer's deep longing for eternal life in the presence of God. Rooted in biblical passages such as 2 Corinthians 5:8—which speaks of being away from the body and at home with the Lord—the song reflects the theological theme of earthly life as a temporary pilgrimage. Ultimately, it serves as a devotional reminder to maintain an eternal perspective, shifting focus away from mortal struggles and toward the promised restoration of heaven.
