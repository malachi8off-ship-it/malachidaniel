Leave the lamp on Lord
Gravel & Grace
meaning
"Leave the lamp on Lord" by Gravel & Grace is a contemporary Christian worship song that captures the themes of repentance, spiritual return, and unwavering divine grace. Drawing deeply from the New Testament parable of the Prodigal Son found in Luke 15, the song portrays a believer seeking to return to God's presence, relying on the imagery of an unquenched light symbolizing the Father's constant readiness to welcome back wayward children. Through its reflective lyrics and acoustic arrangement, the piece emphasizes the themes of forgiveness, spiritual homecoming, and the enduring nature of divine mercy.
