Jayam thannu nadathunna daivathin bhujam
Back to the Cross
meaning
This Christian worship song, sung in the Malayalam language, focuses on the themes of divine victory, healing, and absolute protection provided by the sovereign arm of God. The lyrics refer to God as the ultimate healer and comfort in times of isolation, directly echoing the biblical imagery of the healing balm of Gilead in Jeremiah 8:22 and Christ's assurance to John on the island of Patmos in Revelation 1:17. It highlights the triumphant return of Jesus as King and the ultimate victory believers possess through His enduring strength.
