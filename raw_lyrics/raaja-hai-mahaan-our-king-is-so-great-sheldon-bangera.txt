Raaja Hai Mahaan (Our King Is So Great)
Sheldon Bangera
meaning
Sung in Hindi, this track is a joyful, communal declaration of the majesty and unmatched greatness of God. It draws on the biblical imagery of God's sovereign rule and the call for all nations to acknowledge His kingship.
