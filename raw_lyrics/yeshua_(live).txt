Yeshua (Live)
Jesus Image
meaning
"Yeshua (Live)" by Jesus Image is a contemporary Christian worship song centered on continuous, ecstatic adoration of Jesus, drawing its core meaning from the Hebrew name for Jesus, which translates to "salvation." Musically and lyrically building in intensity, the track reflects the biblical themes of eternal praise and the heavenly throne room described in the Book of Revelation, where elders and living creatures unceasingly exalt the Lamb. Through repetitive, passionate declarations of the name "Yeshua," the song encourages believers to enter into a posture of intimate devotion and spiritual breakthrough.
