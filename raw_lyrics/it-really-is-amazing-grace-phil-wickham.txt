It Really Is Amazing Grace
Phil Wickham
meaning
“It Really Is Amazing Grace” by Phil Wickham is a contemporary worship anthem that celebrates the transformative power of God’s grace, emphasizing how it redeems and restores believers. The lyrics draw directly from biblical themes such as Ephesians 2:8‑9 and Romans 5:8, underscoring that salvation is a free gift of grace, not earned by works, and that this grace empowers Christians to live in gratitude and obedience. The song’s uplifting melody and repetitive refrain invite congregants to reflect on the profound, unmerited love that God extends to humanity.
