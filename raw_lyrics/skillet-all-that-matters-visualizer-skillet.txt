Skillet - All That Matters (Visualizer)
Skillet
meaning
This hard rock track focuses on the ultimate priority of faith in Christ when everything else in the world falls apart. It echoes the Apostle Paul's sentiment in Philippians 3, considering everything a loss compared to the surpassing worth of knowing Christ.
