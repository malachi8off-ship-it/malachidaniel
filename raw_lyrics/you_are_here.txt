You Are Here
Nathaniel Bassey
meaning
"You Are Here" by Nathaniel Bassey is a contemporary Christian worship song that celebrates the omnipresence, sovereignty, and miraculous power of God. Drawing upon biblical themes such as Psalm 139—which emphasizes that God is present in every circumstance and location—the lyrics encourage believers to acknowledge His active presence in their lives for comfort, healing, and spiritual renewal. Through its uplifting melody and congregational worship style, the song serves as a reminder of faith, urging listeners to trust in God's unfailing nearness during both times of trial and celebration.
