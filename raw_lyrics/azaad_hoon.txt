Azaad Hoon
Prince Robinson
meaning
The Christian worship song "Azaad Hoon" by Prince Robinson celebrates the profound spiritual freedom and redemption found through faith in Jesus Christ. Drawing primarily from biblical themes in the New Testament—such as 2 Corinthians 3:17 ("Where the Spirit of the Lord is, there is freedom") and Romans 6—the song emphasizes liberation from the bondage of sin, fear, and spiritual death. Through its uplifting melody and lyrics, it serves as a personal declaration of a believer's new identity and deliverance secured by God's grace.
