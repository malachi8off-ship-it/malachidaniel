Lauren Daigle - Love Like This (Audio)
Lauren Daigle
meaning
This track explores the overwhelming, incomprehensible nature of God's love that pursues us even when we are unlovable. It draws on the theology of Romans 5, marveling at a love that dies for the ungodly.
