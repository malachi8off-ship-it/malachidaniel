Kaisa Pita Ka Pyaar Hai
Sheldon Bangera
meaning
Sung in Hindi, this worship song marvels at the depth, sacrifice, and unconditional nature of God the Father's love. It draws directly from 1 John 3:1, 'See what great love the Father has lavished on us, that we should be called children of God!'
