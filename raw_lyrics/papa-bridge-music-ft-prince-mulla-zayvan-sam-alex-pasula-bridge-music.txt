Papa | Bridge Music ft. Prince Mulla, Zayvan & Sam Alex Pasula
Bridge Music
meaning
Sung in Hinglish, this intimate song expresses the deep, familial love a believer has for God as a perfect Father. It draws directly on the biblical concept found in Romans 8, where the Spirit allows us to cry out, 'Abba, Father.'
