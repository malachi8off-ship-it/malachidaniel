GOODBYE YESTERDAY | Elevation Rhythm
ELEVATION RHYTHM
meaning
GOODBYE YESTERDAY is a contemporary worship track by Elevation Rhythm that encourages believers to let go of past hurts and embrace God’s new purpose for their lives. The lyrics draw on biblical themes of renewal and transformation, referencing passages such as Isaiah 43:18‑19 and 2 Corinthians 5:17 to illustrate the promise of a fresh start. The song invites listeners to trust in God’s power to heal and move forward with hope and confidence.
