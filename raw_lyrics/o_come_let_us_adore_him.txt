O Come Let Us Adore Him
Hillsong Worship
meaning
'O Come Let Us Adore Him' is a Christian worship song by Hillsong Worship, based on the traditional Christmas carol 'O Come, All Ye Faithful' and the hymn 'Hark! The Herald Angels Sing'. The song's core meaning revolves around the adoration and worship of Jesus Christ as the Savior and King, drawing from biblical references such as Luke 2:10-11, where the angels announce the birth of Jesus to the shepherds. This song is typically sung in English, and its message of reverence and devotion to Jesus remains a central theme in Christian worship services around the world.
