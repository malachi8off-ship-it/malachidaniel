Viduthal Ennum Prapikkam
7 Trumpets
meaning
"Viduthal Ennum Prapikkam" is a Christian worship song by 7 Trumpets sung in Malayalam, centered on the theme of divine freedom and spiritual deliverance. Rooted in biblical passages that proclaim liberty to the captives, the song celebrates the liberating power and victory found in Jesus Christ. Through its uplifting melody and congregational style, it encourages believers to experience and proclaim the fullness of God's grace and freedom in their lives.
