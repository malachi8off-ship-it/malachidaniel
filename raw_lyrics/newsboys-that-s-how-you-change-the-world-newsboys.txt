Newsboys - That's How You Change The World
Newsboys
meaning
This song emphasizes that world transformation happens not through massive movements, but through small, everyday acts of Christ-like love and kindness. It reflects the biblical principle of the Kingdom of God growing like a mustard seed through humble service.
