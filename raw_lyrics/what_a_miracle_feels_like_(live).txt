What A Miracle Feels Like (Live)
Tauren Wells
meaning
"What A Miracle Feels Like (Live)" by Tauren Wells is a contemporary Christian worship song that celebrates the awe-inspiring power of divine intervention and spiritual renewal in everyday life. Rooted in biblical themes of resurrection, healing, and transformation—echoing passages such as Ephesians 2:4-5 regarding God's great mercy—the lyrics encourage believers to recognize and praise God for unexpected breakthroughs. Through an uplifting and emotive live musical arrangement, the song directs congregational focus toward experiencing the tangible presence and restorative grace of God.
