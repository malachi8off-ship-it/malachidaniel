Still - Hillsong Worship
Hillsong Worship
meaning
This classic worship song is a beautiful declaration of finding peace and rest under the protective wings of God during life's storms. It perfectly encapsulates Psalm 46:10, 'Be still, and know that I am God,' finding quiet trust amidst chaos.
