Casting Crowns
meaning
"Who Am I" by Casting Crowns is a reflective worship song that explores the believer’s identity in Christ, emphasizing humility, gratitude, and the transformative power of God’s love. Drawing on biblical themes such as Psalm 139’s affirmation of God’s intimate knowledge of us and 1 John 3:1’s declaration of our divine inheritance, the song invites listeners to recognize that their worth and purpose are rooted in being children of God rather than in worldly achievements.
