Mi Oración (La Niña de Tus Ojos) (feat. Melody Adorno)
Maverick City Music
meaning
Sung in Spanish, this intimate song reflects on being deeply loved and protected by God as the 'apple of His eye.' It draws directly from the beautiful imagery found in Psalm 17:8, asking God for tender care and protection.
