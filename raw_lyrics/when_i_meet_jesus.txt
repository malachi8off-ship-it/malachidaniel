When I Meet Jesus
Jeremy Rosado
meaning
"When I Meet Jesus" by Jeremy Rosado is a contemporary Christian worship song that anticipates the ultimate believer's experience of standing in the presence of God in heaven. Drawing inspiration from New Testament themes of eternal life and glorification—such as 1 John 3:2, which speaks of seeing Christ as He is—the lyrics express a posture of surrender, awe, and transformative encounter upon reaching the end of earthly life. Through its emotive ballad style, the song serves as a reflection on the beatific vision, emphasizing the Christian hope of personal communion with the Savior.
