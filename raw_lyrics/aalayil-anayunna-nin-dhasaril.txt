Aalayil Anayunna Nin Dhasaril
Abhijith Kollam
meaning
This Christian devotional song is sung in Malayalam and serves as an entrance or gather hymn for church service. Its core meaning focuses on believers assembling in God's holy temple to offer worship and sacrifices of praise. Biblically, it reflects the context of Psalm 100:4, which exhorts believers to enter His gates with thanksgiving and His courts with praise.
