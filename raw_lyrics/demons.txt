Josiah Queen
meaning
"Demons" by Josiah Queen is an upbeat Christian folk-pop song that addresses the universal struggle against spiritual warfare, temptation, and personal doubt. The lyrics emphasize that while earthly struggles and inner turmoil are constant, believers can overcome them through the strength and victory provided by Jesus Christ. Musically and lyrically, the track draws biblical context from passages like Ephesians 6:12—which reminds Christians that their battle is not against flesh and blood, but against spiritual forces—while ultimately pointing to the triumph of faith over fear.
