I'm Not Ashamed
Newsboys
meaning
This bold rock song is a public declaration of unashamed faith in the Gospel of Jesus Christ. It is a direct musical adaptation of Romans 1:16, where Paul states he is not ashamed of the gospel, for it is the power of God for salvation.
