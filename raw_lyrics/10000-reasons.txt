10,000 Reasons (Bless the Lord)
Matt Redman
meaning
Released by Matt Redman in 2012, "10,000 Reasons (Bless the Lord)" is a contemporary Christian worship song that encourages believers to offer perpetual praise and gratitude to God regardless of their life circumstances. Drawing heavy inspiration from the Psalms—particularly Psalm 103, which opens with the command to "Bless the Lord, O my soul"—the lyrics reflect on themes of divine mercy, unfailing love, and the limits of human mortality compared to eternal worship.
