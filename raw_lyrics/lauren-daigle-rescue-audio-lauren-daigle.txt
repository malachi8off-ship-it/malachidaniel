meaning
"Rescue" by Lauren Daigle is a Christian worship song that conveys a message of hope and salvation, drawing from biblical themes of deliverance and redemption. The song's lyrics reflect the idea that God is always present and willing to rescue those who call out to Him, referencing Psalms 34:17-20, which speaks of God's deliverance from trouble. With a focus on the emotional and spiritual aspects of rescue, the song serves as a heartfelt expression of faith and trust in God's power to save.
