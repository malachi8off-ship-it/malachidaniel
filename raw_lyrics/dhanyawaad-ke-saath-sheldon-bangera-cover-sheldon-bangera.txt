Dhanyawaad Ke Saath - Sheldon Bangera | COVER
Sheldon Bangera
meaning
Sung in Hindi, this classic worship cover is a song of entering God's presence with a heart full of thanksgiving. It is a direct translation and thematic application of Psalm 100:4, entering His gates with thanksgiving.
