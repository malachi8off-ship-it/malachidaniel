Chain Breaker
Zach Williams
meaning
This song presents Jesus as the ultimate deliverer from the chains of addiction, pain, and sin. It draws upon the biblical promises of freedom in Christ, echoing Galatians 5:1 that it is for freedom that Christ has set us free.
