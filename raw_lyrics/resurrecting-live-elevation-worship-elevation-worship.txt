Resurrecting | Live | Elevation Worship
Elevation Worship
meaning
This anthem praises the resurrected Christ, who defeated death and now uses that same power to resurrect dead hearts today. It reflects the theology of Romans 8:11, stating that the Spirit of Him who raised Jesus from the dead dwells within believers.
