Do You Hear Me Lord
Gravel & Grace
meaning
"Do You Hear Me Lord" by Gravel & Grace is a contemporary Christian worship song that captures the universal human experience of feeling isolated and crying out for divine attention during times of personal trial. Rooted in the biblical laments found in the Psalms—such as Psalm 13 and Psalm 22, where writers honestly express feelings of abandonment while ultimately reaffirming their trust in God—the song serves as a musical prayer for reassurance and divine presence. Through its emotive lyrics, the track reassures listeners that questioning God's silence is a valid part of faith and that spiritual endurance is found in the persistent pursuit of a relationship with the Creator.
