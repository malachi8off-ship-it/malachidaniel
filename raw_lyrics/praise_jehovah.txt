Praise Jehovah
Beverly Crawford
meaning
"Praise Jehovah" is an energetic contemporary gospel song by Beverly Crawford that calls believers to offer uninhibited praise and thanksgiving to God. Drawing its core message from biblical themes found throughout the Book of Psalms—such as Psalm 150, which urges everything that has breath to praise the Lord—the song emphasizes joyful adoration as a foundational act of worship regardless of circumstance. Through its uplifting tempo and fervent vocal delivery, the track serves as a spiritual reminder of God's sovereignty and the believer's duty to exalt His holy name.
