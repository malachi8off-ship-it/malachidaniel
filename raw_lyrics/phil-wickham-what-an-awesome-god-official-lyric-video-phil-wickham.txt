meaning
Phil Wickham’s “What An Awesome God” is a contemporary worship anthem that celebrates the majesty, holiness, and creative power of God, inviting listeners to lift their voices in awe and reverence. Drawing on biblical imagery from passages such as Psalm 145:3, Isaiah 45:5, and Revelation 4:11, the song underscores God’s sovereignty over all creation and His steadfast faithfulness, encouraging believers to respond with worship and gratitude. The lyrics combine poetic praise with a call to recognize God’s supreme authority, making it a popular choice for corporate worship and personal devotion alike.
