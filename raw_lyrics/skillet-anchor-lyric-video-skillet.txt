Skillet - Anchor (Lyric Video)
Skillet
meaning
This song is a desperate plea for God to be the stabilizing force in the midst of life's violent storms and anxieties. It draws upon the biblical metaphor found in Hebrews 6:19, describing hope in God as a firm and secure anchor for the soul.
