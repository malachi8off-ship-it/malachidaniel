Everyday Hallelujah
Phil Wickham
meaning
"Everyday Hallelujah" by Phil Wickham is a contemporary worship song that celebrates the continual presence of God’s grace and the joy of living in His love. The lyrics emphasize gratitude for God’s faithfulness and encourage believers to respond with praise in all circumstances, echoing biblical themes of worship found in Psalms (e.g., Psalm 100) and the New Testament call to “give thanks in all circumstances” (1  Thessalonians 5:18). The song’s upbeat melody and repetitive refrain reinforce the idea that worship is not limited to special occasions but is a daily, heartfelt response to God’s enduring mercy.
