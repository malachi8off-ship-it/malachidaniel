2 STEP THE ENEMY
Forrest Frank, LOST & FOUND, ZVC
meaning
"2 STEP THE ENEMY" by Forrest Frank, LOST & FOUND, and ZVC is a Christian worship song that encourages believers to confidently confront and overcome spiritual opposition. The core meaning centers on actively engaging in spiritual warfare, metaphorically "two-stepping" through challenges with joy and assurance, rather than fear. This message is rooted in biblical contexts that highlight God's ultimate victory over evil and the power believers have in Christ to triumph over spiritual adversaries.
