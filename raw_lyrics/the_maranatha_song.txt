The Maranatha Song
William McDowell
meaning
"The Maranatha Song" by William McDowell is a contemporary Christian worship anthem centered on the earnest longing for the return of Jesus Christ and the establishment of God's eternal kingdom. Drawing heavily from New Testament eschatology—specifically the Aramaic phrase *maranatha* ("Come, Lord," found in 1 Corinthians 16:22) and the concluding prayers of the Book of Revelation—the song functions as an expression of spiritual readiness, perseverance through present trials, and ultimate devotion. Musically and lyrically, it guides congregations in corporate reflection and hopeful anticipation of the final fulfillment of Christian redemptive history.
